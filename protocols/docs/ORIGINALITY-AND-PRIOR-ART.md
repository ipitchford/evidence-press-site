# Originality and prior-art boundary

**Search date:** 2026-08-08
**Status:** bounded landscape review; not a systematic literature, patent or
trademark search.

## Search route and limits

The review searched official specifications, public repositories, government and
OECD adoption material, and targeted scholarly/working-paper records. Query
families included:

- `portable agent skill workflow specification YAML JSON evaluation`;
- `AI agent workflow protocol registry business process`;
- `SME AI adoption barriers skills use case trial`;
- `agent protocol evaluation human productivity control group`;
- `AI governance SME self assessment NIST ISO 42001`; and
- the exact proposed object names (`Productivity Protocols`, `Verified Agent Work
  Protocol`, `goal-to-verified-deliverable`).

Official/primary sources were preferred for standards and software. Empirical
claims were traced to the underlying report or paper where accessible. The
search was English-language, time-bounded and not database-systematic; it did not
cover patents, trademarks, paid consultancy libraries or non-public company
methods. Fast-moving 2026 specifications and preprints require rechecking before
publication.

## Exact candidate contribution

Productivity Protocols is testing this contribution:

> A low-experience company can start from one routine workflow, pass a bounded
> suitability and governance screen, use a no-install formative protocol, and retain a
> stage-appropriate evidence chain: formative usability first, feasibility next,
> and only then a separately justified controlled agent-only versus
> protocol-guided evaluation. Each stage licenses only a continue, revise, or
> stop decision within its declared boundary.

The feasibility implementation is not no-install: a facilitator needs Node 18+
and competence with structured files and hashes. It requires no agent-system
integration, while participants can remain in a document-and-chat workflow.

No claim of global novelty or priority is made. A bounded search found no exact
end-to-end equivalent, but absence from this search is not evidence of absence.

## What is established prior art

| Existing contribution | Closest source | Consequence here |
|---|---|---|
| Portable instruction/skill packs with `SKILL.md`, scripts, references, assets and tool declarations | [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification) | Adopt the format; do not claim the pack structure as original. |
| Platform-neutral agents and structured business-process flows represented in JSON/YAML and executed through adapters | [Oracle Open Agent Spec](https://github.com/oracle/agent-spec) | `protocol.yaml` is an adoption/evidence profile, not a new workflow DSL. Map executable flows instead of competing with them. |
| Business-readable process notation | [OMG BPMN](https://www.omg.org/bpmn/) | Treat the kernel as a bounded agent-use control pattern, not a replacement for process modelling. |
| Plan–do–check–act and quality planning | [ISO process approach guidance](https://www.iso.org/files/live/sites/isoorg/files/archive/pdf/en/iso9001-2015-process-appr.pdf) | State inheritance from established quality practice. |
| Agent evaluation, tracing and human calibration | [Anthropic agent-eval guidance](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents), [Microsoft Agent Framework evaluation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/agent-framework/agents/evaluation) | Evaluation mechanics are infrastructure; the research question is incremental business value. |
| Organisational AI risk management | [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework), [UK AI Management Essentials](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/ai-management-essentials-tool/guidance-for-using-the-ai-management-essentials-tool), [NCSC secure AI guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/guidelines-secure-ai-system-development/guidelines) | Crosswalk to established controls and explicitly deny certification/compliance claims. |

## Evidence that the problem matters

The [OECD SME adoption review](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/12/ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises_9c48eae6/426399c1-en.pdf)
identifies skills as a recurring adoption barrier and distinguishes AI novices
from more mature adopters. UK [AI adoption
research](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-adoption-research/ai-adoption-research)
and the [SME Digital Adoption Taskforce](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/sme-digital-adoption-taskforce-final-report/sme-digital-adoption-taskforce-final-report)
likewise point to difficulty identifying use cases, limited skills, information
overload and the value of simple guidance and trials. These are motivation and
design evidence; they do not prove this project will solve the problem.

Published productivity results also caution against a universal benefit claim.
Effects vary by task, worker and system: a large customer-support study reported
gains concentrated among less-experienced workers, while studies of tasks outside
an AI capability frontier and experienced open-source developers report errors or
slowdowns. See [Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/140/2/889/7990658),
[Dell'Acqua et al.](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/orsc.2025.21838)
and the [METR randomized study](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/).

## Novelty tests before stronger language

Before describing the project as a new method rather than a distinctive
synthesis, a future review must:

1. compare the full field-trial profile with Agent Spec, BPMN and major agent
   evaluation/context specifications field by field;
2. search adoption-science, human–computer interaction and implementation-science
   databases, not only agent tooling;
3. inspect open-source workflow/template registries and consultancy playbooks;
4. record exact query strings, databases, dates and screening decisions; and
5. ask independent domain reviewers whether the residual contribution is
   non-trivial.

Until then, public wording is: **an open, evidence-bearing synthesis designed for
novice-company agent trials**.
