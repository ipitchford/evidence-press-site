# Governance crosswalk for a first company trial

This crosswalk helps a small company ask the right questions. It is not an audit,
certification, legal opinion or claim of compliance with NIST, ISO/IEC 42001, UK
AI Management Essentials, the EU AI Act or any sector regime.

| Trial control | Project record | Related external guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Named workflow owner and decision right | pilot plan: owner, approver, stop authority | NIST AI RMF Govern; UK AI Management Essentials accountability |
| Bounded task, intended users and affected people | suitability record | NIST Map 1–5 |
| Data class, destination, retention and deletion | data boundary | NIST Map 4; NCSC secure design/development |
| Same-agent comparator and registered metrics | study plan | NIST Measure 1–2 |
| Human review before any use | permission/approval record | NIST Map 3.5 and Manage controls |
| Errors, near misses, support and incident contact | event log | NIST Manage; NCSC secure operation/maintenance |
| Supplier/model/version dependencies | environment record | NIST third-party risk; NCSC supply-chain security |
| Continue, revise or stop with rationale | decision record | NIST Manage and continuous improvement |
| 30/90-day drift and sustained-use check | follow-up record | NIST Measure 2.4; ongoing monitoring |

Primary sources:

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)
- [NIST AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/)
- [UK AI Management Essentials guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/ai-management-essentials-tool/guidance-for-using-the-ai-management-essentials-tool)
- [NCSC Guidelines for secure AI system development](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/guidelines-secure-ai-system-development/guidelines)
- [ISO/IEC 42001 overview](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001)

Before any real trial, the company remains responsible for identifying applicable
law, contracts, worker consultation, information governance and sector rules.
