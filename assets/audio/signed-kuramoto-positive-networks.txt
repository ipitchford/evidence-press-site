Picture a network of identical oscillators, such as metronomes on a shared table, each nudging its neighbours. Usually every link pulls neighbours into step. A resting state is an arrangement where all the nudges cancel, and it is stable if small disturbances stay small. The limitations: everything happens at an exact, symmetric balance, and generic small changes destroy it.
