The Policy Identification Observatory is a specialized research initiative designed to audit the validity of evidence used in public policy claims. Using an agent-native framework, the project employs artificial intelligence to systematically test whether available data can truly distinguish between proposed causal mechanisms and alternative explanations. Unlike traditional studies that prioritize positive results, this observatory emphasizes transparency and durability by documenting failures, non-identification results, and partial findings. Each case follows a rigid nine-stage protocol that includes adversarial verification and deterministic replay to ensure consistency and rigor. By providing robust-decision maps and identifying informational gaps, the program helps decision-makers understand which choices remain defensible under uncertainty. Ultimately, the institution serves as a blueprint for machine-readable science, prioritizing institutional memory and open-source refutation over simple publication volume.
