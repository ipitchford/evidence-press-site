Error-correcting codes protect messages by making valid messages different from one another. Their minimum distance counts the fewest symbol changes separating two valid messages. A large distance provides room to detect or correct corruption. Algebraic symmetry can make a code compact to describe. But this research candidate shows that one particular kind of symmetry can also impose a hidden weakness.
