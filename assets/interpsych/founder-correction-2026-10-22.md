# Founder clarification: the contested New York incorporation

Source T01. Ian Pitchford, statement supplied in this research conversation on 22 October 2026, after reading the initial history.

“The New York incorporation by Goldhagen is the thing Bob and I went to New York to contest.”

In this context, Bob is Robert M. Young, whose published account of the New York confrontation was discussed in the initial history.

The clarification identifies the incorporation by Goldhagen as the object of Pitchford and Young's trip and challenge. The revised history therefore treats the incorporation within the governance conflict, rather than presenting it as an agreed stage in the network's formal development.

This is attributed first-person historical testimony supplied by the founder. It does not supply an exact travel date, original meeting records, court proceedings or the legal disposition of the challenge. The public filing record independently dates the incorporation to 23 March 1995; Young's published account describes an organisational confrontation and settlement without explicitly identifying the corporate filing.

The narrative, chronology, people index, source catalogue, research notes, PDF and research package have been revised to reflect this clarification. The earlier draft's separation of incorporation from the dispute has been withdrawn. No new external review is claimed for this revision.
