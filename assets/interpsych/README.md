# InterPsych research package

Open **interpsych-history.html** for the complete reading copy, chronology, people index, searchable sources and research notes. It works offline; external citations need a connection.

- **interpsych-history.md**: editable narrative.
- **interpsych-history.pdf**: printable narrative with clickable citations.
- **source-catalogue.csv / .json**: 112 records, comprising 111 online document URLs and the founder clarification, with dates, findings, limitations and access details.
- **founder-correction-2026-10-22.md**: Pitchford’s statement that Goldhagen’s incorporation was what he and Young went to New York to contest; basis for the revised account.
- **timeline.csv**: 36 chronological entries with source links.
- **people.csv**: 32 selective role records.
- **retrieval-inventory.csv**: public-page retrieval inventory, including failures and byte checksums where recorded. This is not a count of independent sources.
- **research-method.md**: coverage, access gaps, dating problems and unresolved questions.

Reconstructed from public online evidence on 22 October 2026 and revised with the founder’s clarification on the same date. Full copyrighted source texts and personal address fields are not included. AI-assisted research and internal review are not external peer review. The incorporation is treated as contested; its filing does not establish agreement by the founder or network, and the legal outcome of the challenge remains undocumented here.
