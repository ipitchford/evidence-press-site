# InterPsych research package

Open **interpsych-history.html** for the complete reading copy, chronology, people index, searchable sources and research notes. It works offline; external citations need a connection.

- **interpsych-history.md**: editable narrative.
- **interpsych-history.pdf**: printable narrative with clickable citations.
- **source-catalogue.csv / .json**: 396 records: 111 from the first investigation, 106 added on 30 October 2026, including 44 archived Sheffield forum pages, and 179 added on 31 October 2026, with dates, findings, limitations and access details.
- **timeline.csv**: 64 chronological entries with source links.
- **people.csv**: 71 selective role records.
- **retrieval-inventory.csv**: public-page retrieval inventory, including failures and byte checksums where recorded. This is not a count of independent sources.
- **graphics/**: two original InterPsych graphics, the website logo and the affiliation badge, recovered from 2000 captures.
- **research-method.md**: coverage, access gaps, dating problems and unresolved questions.

Reconstructed from public online evidence on 22 October 2026 and extended on 30 and 31 October 2026. Full copyrighted source texts and personal address fields are not included. AI-assisted research and internal review are not external peer review. Commissioned by InterPsych's founder, Ian Pitchford, a participant in the events described. The records recovered do not show who authorised the 1995 New York incorporation or how it related to the 1995 dispute; no record of legal proceedings concerning the corporation has been found.
