# InterPsych research package

Open **interpsych-history.html** for the complete reading copy, chronology, people index, searchable sources and research notes. It works offline; external citations need a connection.

- **interpsych-history.md**: editable narrative.
- **interpsych-history.pdf**: printable narrative with clickable citations.
- **source-catalogue.csv / .json**: 217 records: 111 from the first investigation and 106 added on 30 October 2026, including 44 archived Sheffield forum pages, with dates, findings, limitations and access details.
- **timeline.csv**: 47 chronological entries with source links.
- **people.csv**: 68 selective role records.
- **retrieval-inventory.csv**: public-page retrieval inventory, including failures and byte checksums where recorded. This is not a count of independent sources.
- **graphics/**: two original InterPsych graphics, the website logo and the affiliation badge, recovered from 2000 captures.
- **research-method.md**: coverage, access gaps, dating problems and unresolved questions.

Reconstructed from public online evidence on 22 October 2026 and extended on 30 October 2026. Full copyrighted source texts and personal address fields are not included. AI-assisted research and internal review are not external peer review. The incorporation is treated as contested; its filing does not establish agreement by the founder or network, and the legal outcome of the challenge remains undocumented here.
