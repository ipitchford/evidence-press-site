Privacy and terms

Your choices stay with you.

The atlas has no visitor account or analytics tracker. Book links marked “Ad” are Amazon advertising links.

Advertising links

The shop and the “Books on this topic” lists contain Amazon advertising links, each group marked “Ad”. As an Amazon Associate, Evidence Press earns from qualifying purchases. The atlas does not record which links you follow and places no advertising cookies. When you follow a link, Amazon receives the request and applies its own privacy and cookie practices. Books are selected editorially; no author or publisher pays to be listed.

Your library

Watch later, likes, hidden records, watched status, playlists and path progress are saved in this browser’s local storage. They are not sent to Evidence Press or YouTube. Export creates a file on your device; import reads a file you choose. Reset removes this atlas’s library data from this browser. Browser storage can be cleared by your browser and does not sync automatically.

Video providers

External players and thumbnails load only when you request them. Doing so contacts the named provider and reveals your IP address and browser information. Players may use cookies, collect further information during playback and display advertisements. Opening a source link contacts that source directly. No player autoplays.

YouTube

YouTube links open the original provider. The public catalogue uses independently sourced institutional information; it does not publish the maintainer’s temporary YouTube API metadata cache. YouTube players use privacy-enhanced mode and load only after you choose them. An embed option is not confirmation that the provider currently permits playback; use the permanent source link if a player is restricted or unavailable. By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and acknowledge the Google Privacy Policy. Visitors do not authorise this atlas to access their YouTube account.

Local likes and progress are atlas preferences, not YouTube likes or watch history. Scientific assessments and editorial selections are Evidence Press’s own information, independent of YouTube.

Control

Use My library to export or reset local choices. You can also clear this site’s browser storage. No video is hosted or downloaded by this site.

Corrections and privacy questions

Prepare a correction or privacy request for Evidence Press. This form prepares a file on your device; it does not send your report. Hosting providers receive the ordinary connection information needed to serve the website.
