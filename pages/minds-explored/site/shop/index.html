Cells, circuits and brains

  • Behave Robert Sapolsky
    One behaviour traced back through seconds, hormones, development and evolution.
  • The Idea of the Brain Matthew Cobb
    How each era's best metaphor shaped what was found, and what it missed.
  • The Brain: The Story of You David Eagleman
    A short, clear first tour of how a brain builds a person.
  • Phantoms in the Brain V. S. Ramachandran
    Single patients used as experiments on how the brain maps the body.
  • Neuroscience: Exploring the Brain Mark Bear
    The standard first textbook when popular books stop being enough.

Perception, attention and action

  • The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat Oliver Sacks
    Case histories that show perception is assembled, not received.
  • The Invisible Gorilla Christopher Chabris
    What attention experiments show we miss, and why we feel sure we don't.
  • The Experience Machine Andy Clark
    The case that perception is prediction corrected by the senses.
  • Eye and Brain Richard Gregory
    A classic on vision as hypothesis-testing, with the illusions to prove it.
  • Deviate Beau Lotto
    Why we never see the world as it is, told through visual experiments.

Learning, memory and education

  • Make It Stick Peter Brown
    What testing and spacing experiments say about study that lasts.
  • The Seven Sins of Memory Daniel Schacter
    How memory fails, and why those failures are the cost of a useful system.
  • Why Don't Students Like School? Daniel Willingham
    A cognitive scientist's answers to questions teachers actually ask.
  • In Search of Memory Eric Kandel
    A Nobel laureate's account of finding memory in synapses.
  • A Mind for Numbers Barbara Oakley
    Practical study methods for subjects that feel hard.

Language and communication

  • The Language Instinct Steven Pinker
    The strongest popular case for language as a biological adaptation.
  • Through the Language Glass Guy Deutscher
    A careful look at when language does and doesn't shape thought.
  • Proust and the Squid Maryanne Wolf
    How the brain learns to read, and what happens when it struggles.
  • The Power of Babel John McWhorter
    How languages change, split and mix over time.
  • How Language Works David Crystal
    A reference-style guide from speech sounds to meaning.

Reasoning and decisions

  • Thinking, Fast and Slow Daniel Kahneman
    The source text on judgement, heuristics and bias.
  • Superforecasting Philip Tetlock
    What separates accurate forecasters from confident ones.
  • Risk Savvy Gerd Gigerenzer
    How to read risks and frequencies, with a challenge to the bias view.
  • Noise Daniel Kahneman
    Why expert judgements of the same case scatter, and how to reduce it.
  • Misbehaving Richard Thaler
    The making of behavioural economics, told by one of its founders.

Development across life

  • The Gardener and the Carpenter Alison Gopnik
    What developmental science says children need from adults.
  • Inventing Ourselves Sarah-Jayne Blakemore
    The adolescent brain, from the scientist who mapped much of it.
  • The Scientist in the Crib Alison Gopnik
    How infants learn about people, objects and words.
  • Age of Opportunity Laurence Steinberg
    Adolescence as a second period of heightened plasticity.
  • Successful Aging Daniel Levitin
    What changes in the ageing brain, and what the evidence supports doing.

Emotion, motivation and sleep

  • How Emotions Are Made Lisa Feldman Barrett
    The constructionist challenge to the idea of built-in emotion circuits.
  • Why Zebras Don't Get Ulcers Robert Sapolsky
    Stress physiology and health, explained with unusual clarity.
  • Life Time Russell Foster
    Body clocks and sleep from a leading circadian scientist.
  • Good Habits, Bad Habits Wendy Wood
    The research on how habits form and why intentions lose to them.
  • Anxious Joseph LeDoux
    What threat circuits do, and why that is not the same as feeling afraid.

Personality and individual differences

  • Blueprint Robert Plomin
    A behavioural geneticist's case for how DNA shapes who we are.
  • The Genetic Lottery Kathryn Paige Harden
    Why genetic differences matter for social equality.
  • Intelligence: All That Matters Stuart Ritchie
    A short, evidence-led guide to what intelligence tests measure.
  • Personality: What Makes You the Way You Are Daniel Nettle
    The five personality dimensions and their costs and benefits.
  • Me, Myself, and Us Brian Little
    Personality science applied to everyday lives and projects.

  • Influence Robert Cialdini

  • Becoming Human Michael Tomasello
    How shared intention makes human development distinctive.

    The mathematical foundations of computational neuroscience.
