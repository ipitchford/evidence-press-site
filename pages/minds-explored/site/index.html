From a brain question to an experiment

Follow the Human Brain course from its central questions through methods, experimental design and competing interpretations. The route preserves the relevant lecture order and adds a continuing task: refine one question into two discriminating experiments.

6 steps · Learning path

Development from behaviour to brain and study design

Compare two introductory accounts of child development, extend the question across adulthood, then move to cortical organisation and the setting in which developmental studies are conducted. The recurring task is to separate a target ability from the demands of its measurement.

→

8 steps · Learning path

From neurons and spike data to network models

Begin with physical and circuit models, connect them to synapses and spike data, then extend to receptive fields and recurrent memory models. Each step asks what the representation includes and what observation would challenge it. This is a selected route, with the full course available for omitted derivations.
