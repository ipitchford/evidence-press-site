Learning path

Comparing minds across species without a single ladder

Move from scaling and task design to development, field behaviour and human origins. Each step changes one part of the comparison so that the final synthesis is a research plan, not a ranking of species.

For: Learners who want to compare brains and behaviour while keeping the task, species and explanatory level explicit.

What you will work towards

  • Distinguish raw size from proportional scaling.
  • Specify the capacity a behavioural task measures.
  • Separate developmental mechanism from evolutionary function.
  • Formulate rival explanations and a discriminating comparison.

Limits of this route

Official paired summaries and session abstracts were inspected for 2019–2025 material, with the specified comparative-methods and animal-consciousness reading sections. The sequence is not a systematic review or a full-video audit. Social-organisation analogies are not treated as identity of psychological mechanisms, and behavioural performance is not a direct measure of subjective experience.
