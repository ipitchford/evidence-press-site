Learning path

Emotion, stress and questions about regulation

Begin with what an emotional episode contains, compare functional and social explanations, and then study stress and regulation as research questions. The route repeatedly asks what was measured and what would count as a change. It does not promise improved wellbeing or teach a treatment package. The final task turns an appealing regulation claim into an evaluable study question.

For: A reader who wants to compare emotional theories and evaluate claims about managing responses.

What you will work towards

  • Distinguish valence, arousal and a reported feeling.
  • Compare immediate mechanisms with functional and evolutionary accounts.
  • Specify a stress measure and its timescale.
  • Describe what evidence would be needed for a regulation-effect claim.
