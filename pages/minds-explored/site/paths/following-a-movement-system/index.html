Learning path

Following a movement system from muscle to brain

Work upwards through the dedicated human-movement course: motor units, spinal circuits and higher systems. The order preserves the relevant official course sequence while omitting unrelated lessons for a manageable first route. Each task adds to one labelled explanation of a hypothetical reach. Anatomical assignments remain tentative until supported; the aim is to connect levels, not memorise a different structure at every stop.

For: A learner seeking an ordered anatomy-and-control route rather than a general brain survey.

What you will work towards

  • Relate a motor unit to neural output and muscle action.
  • Distinguish preparation, feedback and a local reflex question.
  • Place spinal, cortical, cerebellar and basal-ganglia topics in one learning map.
  • State what the inspected course outline can and cannot establish about individual lectures.

Limits of this route

All eight selections were checked against the actual official abstract, syllabus and exact ordered entries. The motor-unit definition and sensory-feedback context also have inspected UTHealth textbook support. Individual lecture transcripts and full recordings were not inspected. The tasks are original questions, not reports of experiments in the lectures. This route does not provide rehabilitation advice.
