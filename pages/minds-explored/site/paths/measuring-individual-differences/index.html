Learning path

What a score says about a person

Compares performance-based and descriptive measures, then asks what their construction permits us to infer.

For: Readers seeking a careful basis for interpreting intelligence and personality measures.

What you will work towards

  • Separate observed responses, test scores, constructs and causal explanations.
  • Compare performance tasks with ratings of characteristic behaviour.
  • Specify what reliability, validity and longitudinal evidence would be needed for an interpretation.

Limits of this route

Yale and MIT introductory teaching dates are 2007 and 2011. NPTEL selections use official outlines and available course descriptions. The two birth-order studies were inspected at summary level only, to avoid repeating a dated answer-key generalisation as settled fact. This route is not an assessment of a person, a group comparison or a current systematic review. This is an agent editorial check, not human peer review or independent scientific assessment.
