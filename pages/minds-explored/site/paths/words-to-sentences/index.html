Learning path

From recognising words to understanding sentences

Tracks linguistic units and processing questions from words through structure to discourse.

For: Readers moving from a general introduction to specific language-processing questions.

What you will work towards

  • Separate a linguistic unit from a process that makes it available.
  • Represent two structural interpretations of the same sentence.
  • Explain what context adds beyond word meanings and sentence structure.

Limits of this route

The official ordered NPTEL outlines establish the intended progression; they do not establish the details of a processing theory or its present scientific standing. The Yale introductory lecture is from 2007. Structural analyses in the exercise are not evidence of a particular brain mechanism.
