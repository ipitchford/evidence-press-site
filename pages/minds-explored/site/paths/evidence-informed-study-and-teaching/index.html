Learning path

From a study attempt to a teaching design

Moves from a learner’s retrieval attempt to an educator’s aligned task and feedback design.

For: Independent learners, tutors and educators connecting study practice with lesson design.

What you will work towards

  • Separate recognition of an explanation from an attempt to recall it.
  • Match an observable learning objective with an appropriate practice and assessment.
  • Write feedback that identifies a specific next action.
