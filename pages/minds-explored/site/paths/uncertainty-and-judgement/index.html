Learning path

Probability models and human judgement

Establishes mathematical standards before considering judgements and selection effects.

For: Learners wanting formal probability foundations before evaluating claims about judgement.

What you will work towards

  • Specify a sample space and the assumptions of a probability model.
  • Compute a conditional probability and recognise an independence assumption.
  • Separate an error relative to a specified model from a disagreement about goals or modelling choices.
