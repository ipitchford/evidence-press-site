Learning path

From close bonds to groups and cultural expectations

Begin with defined relationships, add physiological and field perspectives, then examine groups, culturally valued feelings and policy aims. The path’s recurring task is to make each change of scale explicit.

For: Learners interested in social life across several scales who want to keep biological, contextual and normative explanations distinct.

What you will work towards

  • Define the relationship or collective outcome being explained.
  • Separate candidate mechanisms from ecological and cultural context.
  • Distinguish a report of an intervention from independent evidence of effectiveness.
  • Separate felt emotion, desired emotion and proposed social objectives.

A short starting route

Steps 1, 4, 7. These three steps compare a defined close relationship, its human context and a distinct cultural-emotion question; use the linked readings to retain the conceptual distinctions.

Continue more deeply

Return to the Origin of Love series for a sustained comparison of care and bonding, using the culture-and-emotion reading to keep felt and desired emotion separate. Continue at source ↗

Limits of this route

This route uses 2025 attachment abstracts, 2022–2023 annual-lecture descriptions, an undated institutional media archive and the specified reading sections. Full recordings were not watched. The laboratory archive establishes the Tsai interview’s identity; the conceptual account comes from the inspected readings. No hormone intervention, intergroup intervention or policy framework is endorsed as independently effective.
