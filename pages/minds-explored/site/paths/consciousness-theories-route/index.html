Learning path

From experience to competing theories of consciousness

This sequence first clarifies the target, then contrasts workspace, integrated-information and predictive accounts, and finally examines how measures and models enter an empirical test. It does not promise a settled theory of consciousness.

For: Independent learners who want to move from the basic question of experience to an explicit comparison of research approaches.

What you will work towards

  • Distinguish an experience, a report and a recorded signal.
  • State the scope of an attributed theoretical proposal.
  • Construct a comparison of predictions without equating task performance with experience.
  • Identify assumptions linking a numerical measure or model to a claim.

A short starting route

Steps 1, 2, 3. These steps establish the problem and two explicitly attributed approaches; use the testing-theories guide and 2025 paper before drawing empirical conclusions.

Continue more deeply

Read the 2025 adversarial comparison and its differing interpretations, then return to the Oxford Models of Consciousness series for an advanced continuation. Continue at source ↗

Limits of this route

The route combines 2015 teaching material, 2019 conference proposals and 2024 discussions. Specified descriptions, paired summaries and the 2025 paper’s abstract, predictions and discussion framing were inspected. Full videos and supplementary analyses were not reviewed. The AI application does not establish machine experience; numerical measures are not labelled validated detectors. Steps 5–6 are the advanced branch.
