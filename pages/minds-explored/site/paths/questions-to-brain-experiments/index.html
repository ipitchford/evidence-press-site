Learning path

From a brain question to an experiment

Follow the Human Brain course from its central questions through methods, experimental design and competing interpretations. The route preserves the relevant lecture order and adds a continuing task: refine one question into two discriminating experiments.

For: Learners with introductory psychology who want to understand the logic behind cognitive-neuroscience studies.

What you will work towards

  • Describe a study in terms of question, task, measure and comparison.
  • Explain why different methods bear on different aspects of a question.
  • Propose a follow-up experiment that tests an alternative explanation.
  • Distinguish a relationship between brain regions from a complete account of their interaction.

Limits of this route

The videos come from the 2019 Human Brain collection. Publisher descriptions and the design assignment were inspected, not full lectures. The path’s tasks are paper exercises, not validated study protocols; resource scientific assessment remains unassessed. Course scientific positions should be attributed rather than presented as universal consensus.
