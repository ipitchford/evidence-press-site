Learning path

Design and evaluate a behavioural study

An eight-step route from a population and outcome to a comparison, power assumptions and an interpretable interval. The associated guide and design readings supply the experimental-design concepts that the statistics demonstrations do not cover.

For: A beginning student planning a study on paper before collecting data.

What you will work towards

  • Specify a population, outcome and target quantity.
  • Explain why paired and separate-group measurements require different comparisons.
  • State effect-size and variability assumptions behind a sample-size plan.
  • Describe the planned estimate and uncertainty without promising a positive result.

Limits of this route
