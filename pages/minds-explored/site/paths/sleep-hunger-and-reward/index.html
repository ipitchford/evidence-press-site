Learning path

Sleep, hunger and the circumstances of motivation

Start with Sleep 3, whose official description concerns rhythms and sleep, and the accompanying NIGMS reading; then compare emotion and eating. Sleep 1/2 are omitted from the required sequence because their descriptions discuss consciousness and appear inconsistent with their titles. The route is organised around regulation, cues and measurement, ending with food access so that an explanation of a choice remains connected to its circumstances.

For: A learner interested in how internal states and environments jointly enter explanations of behaviour.

What you will work towards

  • Distinguish a daily behavioural pattern from a proposed clock mechanism.
  • Separate an internal state, a cue and an observed action.
  • Identify measurement uncertainty in eating research.
  • Compare an individual reward model with an environmental-access explanation.

Limits of this route

NPTEL’s Sleep 1/2 descriptions discuss consciousness and appear shifted or inconsistent; they are omitted from required steps and their unsupported numerical mind-division claim is not used. Sleep 3 explicitly describes rhythms and sleep, but no sleep transcript or playback was inspected. API title matches establish identity only. NIGMS supplies the clock account. MIT/Yale resources are historical teaching. No sleep manipulation, diet recommendation or addiction diagnosis is proposed.
