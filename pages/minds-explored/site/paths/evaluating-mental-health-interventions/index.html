Learning path

From a mental-health claim to an evidence question

Move from classification and a specific disorder family to treatment evaluation and the difference between individual and population questions. Read the current NIH/NIMH/NICE companions alongside the older lectures. The final product is a hypothetical evidence table or protocol, not a recommendation about care. Each step asks what would support the claim being made and what remains unexplained.

For: A reader who wants to understand clinical research without making treatment decisions from a video.

What you will work towards

  • Distinguish a diagnostic category from a causal mechanism.
  • Specify a treatment-evidence question with a population, comparator, outcome and follow-up.
  • Explain the purpose and limits of random assignment.
  • Separate individual treatment questions from population-policy questions.
  • Recognise that a research framework or educational video is not an individual assessment.

Limits of this route

All videos in this route are historical 2007/2011 teaching; current clinical boundaries come from the retrieved institutional and guideline readings. The Yale mood lecture has a reported YouTube age restriction and is omitted from required steps. These materials are not a systematic review or treatment ranking. The protocol task is a paper exercise, not permission to recruit participants, diagnose or change care.
