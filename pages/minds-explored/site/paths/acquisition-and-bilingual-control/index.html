Learning path

Learning languages and controlling their use

Separates developmental evidence, acquisition histories and control during language use; avoids a generic cognitive-advantage promise.

For: Students interested in how language-learning history relates to current language use.

What you will work towards

  • Identify an observable response used to study early language knowledge.
  • Keep an acquisition history separate from performance on a present task.
  • Distinguish a language-switching result from evidence of a general cognitive advantage.
