Learning path

Read a quantitative paper critically

A ten-step reading sequence that moves from the recorded variables and descriptive patterns to the tested parameter, uncertainty, model assumptions and prediction claims. Use the linked causal and transparency readings for questions the statistical calculation cannot answer.

For: A reader who wants to evaluate a reported quantitative result without beginning by reproducing every equation.

What you will work towards

  • Identify the recorded variables, denominators and target quantity.
  • Distinguish effect size, p-value and interval uncertainty.
  • Recognise when a regression claim changes from association to prediction or causation.
  • Write a concise assessment with explicit unresolved information.

Limits of this route

The video descriptions were checked against identified official-transcript excerpts, not complete viewing. An accessible official media endpoint is not evidence that every frame played. Teaching examples are not independent validation of the underlying scientific or clinical claims. The route supplies an initial appraisal, not an independent statistical reanalysis. An unavailable method, dataset or analysis plan remains an explicit gap.
