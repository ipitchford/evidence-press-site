Topic guide · Evolution and other minds / Cells, circuits and brains / Methods, evidence and reproducibility

What can—and cannot—we learn by comparing brains across species?

Move from brain size and proportional scaling to cellular development and task-specific behaviour, without turning the comparison into a ranking of intelligence.

Topic guide · Personality and individual differences / Development across life / Methods, evidence and reproducibility / Evolution and other minds

How do genes and environment influence behaviour?

Interpret family and twin comparisons, population heritability and developmental experiments without turning them into percentages of a person or claims of inevitable outcomes. This route explains foundational inference, not personal genetic risk or modern genome-wide prediction.

Topic guide · Social and cultural behaviour / Evolution and other minds / Methods, evidence and reproducibility

How do group identities shape collective behaviour?

Compare identity, norms, incentives and authority as possible explanations, then examine the move from research findings to policy proposals.

Topic guide · Evolution and other minds / Consciousness and philosophy / Methods, evidence and reproducibility

How can we investigate consciousness in other animals?

Compare evidence about experience with evidence about particular behavioural abilities, while making alternative explanations explicit.

↗

Topic guide · Evolution and other minds / Social and cultural behaviour / Emotion, motivation and sleep

How does social attachment work across species?

Distinguish kinds of relationship and connect behavioural, physiological, developmental and ecological questions.

↗

Topic guide · Evolution and other minds / Social and cultural behaviour / Methods, evidence and reproducibility

How can evolutionary explanations of human behaviour be tested?

Separate mechanism, development, ancestry and possible function, then ask which comparative observations could distinguish rival accounts.
