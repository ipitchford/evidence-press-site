How do genes and environment influence behaviour?

Interpret family and twin comparisons, population heritability and developmental experiments without turning them into percentages of a person or claims of inevitable outcomes. This route explains foundational inference, not personal genetic risk or modern genome-wide prediction.

Topic guide · Personality and individual differences / Methods, evidence and reproducibility

How can personality be described without turning traits into types?

Describe traits as dimensions supported by observations, with context and change still visible.

Topic guide · Methods, evidence and reproducibility / Personality and individual differences

How can you tell whether a measure is reliable and valid?

Separate what a score records, how consistently it behaves and which interpretation the evidence supports.

What do intelligence test scores measure?

Distinguish a performance, a score, a construct and a causal explanation when reading intelligence claims.
