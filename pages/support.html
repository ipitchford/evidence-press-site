A few things to know

Why support something everyone can read for free?

Creating and publishing the work has recurring costs. Voluntary contributions help cover those costs while keeping access open. A student could study an argument, a teacher adapt an explanation, or a researcher examine the code. They can benefit without becoming paying members.

What happens if the research does not succeed?

Research is uncertain. Partial results, counterexamples and explanations of failed approaches can still give others something useful to examine. Support funds the work, with no guarantee of a discovery or a particular result.

How is the research checked?

Each release states its own evidence and assurance status. Producer-side checks, independent reproduction, formal verification and peer review answer different questions. Funding does not change those scientific standards or give a result a stronger status.

Does contributing buy access or influence?

Every contribution supports the same public work. Findings, corrections and editorial decisions remain independent of payments. The suggested amounts carry no private research deliverables.

How will supporters hear about progress?

Follow new releases on the Evidence Press website. Each release states its evidence, limitations and research status. Research takes different forms, so support carries no fixed quota of discoveries or films.
