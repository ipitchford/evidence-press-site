Why does merge sort scale differently from insertion sort?

Watch the recursion tree take shape. The amount of work at a single level and the number of levels answer different counting questions.

Topic guide

How can a ray matrix predict the path through a camera lens?

Represent a ray by its height and angle at a reference plane. Propagation and refraction each change that pair. Following the ray element by element makes the ordering of the optical system explicit.

↗

Topic guide

Can a moving camera estimate time to contact from an expanding image?

Follow the projection of an approaching object onto the image plane. A single apparent size confounds size and distance. Its rate of change supplies another piece of evidence under assumptions about motion.
