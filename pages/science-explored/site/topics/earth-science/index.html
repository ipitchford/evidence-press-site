Topic guide

What sets the speed of air–sea gas exchange?

A gas can be far from equilibrium with seawater and still enter it slowly. Listen for the distinction between the concentration the water could support and the rate at which molecules cross the interface.

Topic guide

When does mixing fail to explain estuary chemistry?

Start with two water masses. If mixing is the only process, each dissolved constituent has a concentration fixed by the fraction of each water mass present. A reaction can move an observation away from that prediction.

↗

Topic guide

When does flowing water carry sediment in suspension?

Track the distinction between material travelling near the bed and material carried within the water column. A sampler near the surface and one at the bed can give different impressions of the same flow.

↗

Topic guide

How can layers of rock record the direction of an ancient current?

Begin with a moving bedform. A dune has a shape at an instant and a history of migration. The layers it leaves behind need not preserve the whole shape.
