When can an entire circuit be replaced by two components?

Begin with the junction and loop equations. They let you predict what an external load will measure without observing every internal current.

How does a solar cell turn light into current?

In the photoconductivity tutorial, separate the creation of mobile charge carriers from their movement. An increase in conductivity does not by itself specify which terminal will collect the electrons.

Topic guide

What makes a feedback signal decay or grow?

Follow a single value around a delay loop. The delay determines when it returns and the gain determines how much comes back. A block diagram can make both easier to trace than an expanded sequence.

↗

Topic guide

How can two different tones give the same digital samples?

Oppenheim’s demonstration lets you compare a continuous sinusoid with its values on a sampling grid. A sample records the signal at one instant and says nothing directly about the path between instants.

↗

Topic guide

How can a ray matrix predict the path through a camera lens?

Represent a ray by its height and angle at a reference plane. Propagation and refraction each change that pair. Following the ray element by element makes the ordering of the optical system explicit.
