Topic guide

Can two processes transfer different amounts of heat between the same states?

Listen for how a boundary lets energy cross as heat or work. A state measurement can constrain their combined effect while leaving the history of the process open.

Topic guide

Why does an isolated system approach equilibrium?

The entropy-maximisation argument requires a boundary condition. Track what can move between subsystems, what remains fixed for the whole, and where the extremum is taken.

Topic guide

Why can honeycombs made of the same material have different stiffnesses?

Look at the direction and thickness of the cell walls. An applied force can stretch one wall and bend another, even when both walls are made of the same solid.

Topic guide

How does a solar cell turn light into current?

In the photoconductivity tutorial, separate the creation of mobile charge carriers from their movement. An increase in conductivity does not by itself specify which terminal will collect the electrons.

↗

Topic guide

Why do thin films show different colours at different angles?

Watch for colour changes as viewing geometry changes. Light reflected at the two faces of a film travels along different optical paths. The relative phase depends on the paths and the wavelength.
