When does a repeated observation become a calculation?

Astronomers, physicians, builders and mathematical teachers had different reasons to preserve procedures. Ask what a reader would need to repeat a result away from its author.

In the book’s Contents, open “500 BCE–600 CE · Arguments, instruments and organised knowledge”.

Sky records

Compare Babylonian series of observations with a modern spectrum. Both preserve differences for later comparison, but they measure different properties of the sky.

Episode 8: Spectroscopy · MIT OpenCourseWare

Geometry and water

Archimedes related displacement to a body’s behaviour in water. Sketch the forces on a floating object and identify which quantities an observer could weigh.

A review of Newton's three laws of motion · MIT OpenCourseWare

Paper and built knowledge

Follow the book from papermaking to the movement of technical instructions. Compare the knowledge preserved in a written direction with the tacit judgement needed to make the material itself.

Materials in Human Experience · MIT OpenCourseWare

Calculating with real quantities

Read the St Andrews account of the Chinese Nine Chapters. Take one problem about distributing goods or measuring land. Before calculating, list the quantities known and the one sought.

The Nine Chapters on the Mathematical Art · University of St Andrews, MacTutor

Concrete and architectural form

Trace a load through a Roman vault. How did working with concrete change the shapes a builder could enclose? What can the finished interior tell us about the construction sequence?

Technology and Revolution in Roman Architecture · Open Yale Courses

Medical explanation in antiquity
