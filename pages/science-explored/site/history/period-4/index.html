What changes when knowledge crosses languages?

A translation, an instrument and a set of recorded quantities carry different parts of a practice. Follow examples from the Islamic world, West Africa and the Andes.

Astronomy for a particular place

Use the Met’s teaching on Islamic astronomy to connect an instrument with local time and direction. Which settings must change when the observer moves?

Astronomy and Astrology in the Medieval Islamic World · The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Working buildings

Study the Great Mosque of Djenné as a building maintained by a community. Compare what its surface reveals with what must be learned from the history of replacement and repair. The standing mosque is later than its medieval predecessors.

Great Mosque of Djenné · Smarthistory

Numbers in cords

Examine an Inka khipu. Separate the position and form of a knot from the identity of the goods or people being counted. The book’s Andean sequence extends across the period boundary.

The Inka khipu · Smarthistory

Zero as a mathematical object

Follow the distinctions between an empty position, a written placeholder and a number with arithmetic rules. Ask which of the three a particular inscription can demonstrate.

A history of zero · University of St Andrews, MacTutor

Arabic chemical learning

Compare a surviving Arabic alchemical text with a surviving vessel. Decide what each tells us about the movement of vocabulary and about work actually performed.

Notes on Arabic Alchemy · Science History Institute
