How did print and instruments change who could check a claim?

A printed diagram could reach distant readers. A navigational observation still depended on who took it, where they stood and how the instrument was set.

In the book’s Contents, open “1450–1650 · Print, navigation and new observations”.

Print and anatomy

Compare the repeatability of a printed anatomical figure with the variation between bodies. Which features could later readers check without consulting the same specimen?

Knowledge and ideas in early modern Europe · The Open University

Navigation by sky and sea

Compare a recorded altitude with Pacific wayfinding. Identify which observations depend on the season and which require close familiarity with local conditions.

Hawaiian wayfinding aboard Hōkūleʻa · Smithsonian Center for Folklife and Cultural Heritage

Seeing through glass

Trace two rays from a distant point through a lens. Use the construction to ask how an observer might distinguish an object in the sky from an imperfection in the instrument.

Lecture 3: Focusing, imaging, and the paraxial approximation · MIT OpenCourseWare

Brass, exchange and political authority

Examine a Benin court plaque and the evidence for traded brass. Distinguish the movement of the metal from the local authority, skills and purposes that shaped the object.

Benin and the Portuguese · Smarthistory
