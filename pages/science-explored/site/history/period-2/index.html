How can an institution make a quantity travel?

Early urban societies had to move goods, divide work and keep records that another person could read. An account is evidence both for quantities and for the people who had reason to count.

In the book’s Contents, open “3000–500 BCE · Counting, cultivating and building”.

Barley in an account

Examine the Sumerian tablet that records malt and groats. Which distinctions would matter to the storehouse but disappear in a single total for grain?

Administrative account of malt and barley groats · The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Metal in circulation

The Uluburun cargo brings copper and tin together at sea. A cargo establishes movement; the place where someone cast the finished object needs independent evidence.

Materials in Human Experience · MIT OpenCourseWare

Measuring a night hour

An Egyptian water clock could be marked for use at different times of year. Ask what its graduations say about the definition of an hour.

Telling Time in Ancient Egypt · The Metropolitan Museum of Art

City water and labour
