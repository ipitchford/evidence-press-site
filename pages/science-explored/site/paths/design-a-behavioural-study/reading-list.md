# Design and evaluate a behavioural study

1. [Lecture 06 : Objectives, Steps and Basic Definitions](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/111104120) — Write a target population and a feasible sample; state one limitation in moving from one to the other.
2. [Lecture 07 : Variables and Types of Data](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/111104120) — Define the primary outcome, units, time window and variable type.
3. [Motivation](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/103106120) — State the target difference or proportion in ordinary language and explain what it leaves unanswered.
4. [Statistics for Variance and Proportion](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/103106120) — Explain why a mean, variability or proportion is the appropriate target for your particular question.
5. [Hypothesis testing of means](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/103106120) — Sketch a separate-group and a paired version; read the linked design chapter and identify a carryover issue.
6. [Power of hypothesis test](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/103106120) — Name a meaningful alternative and distinguish it from the null hypothesis.
7. [Factors affecting hypothesis test](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/103106120) — Describe how your plan would change under greater variability or a smaller relevant effect.
8. [Confidence interval construction](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/103106120) — Write a mock results sentence with placeholders for an effect estimate and confidence interval, then one sentence about a remaining design limitation.
