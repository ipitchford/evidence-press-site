# From starlight to stellar ancestry

For astronomy students using light to infer the history of a stellar population. Build an argument from wavelength shift, elemental abundance and enrichment.

1. What can a redshift tell us about motion?
   - [Lecture 3: The Doppler Effect and Special Relativity](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-286-the-early-universe-fall-2013/resources/lecture-3-the-doppler-effect-and-special-relativity/)
   - [Lecture 4: The Kinematics of the Homogeneous Expanding Universe](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-286-the-early-universe-fall-2013/resources/lecture-4-the-kinematics-of-the-homogeneous-expanding-universe/)
   Draw four emitted crests at equal source-time intervals. Increase the distance travelled by each successive crest and mark their arrivals. Identify the assumption that makes this a model of relative motion.
2. Where did the elements in a star come from?
   - [Episode 2. What the Universe Is Made Of](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-8-007-cosmic-origin-of-the-chemical-elements-fall-2019/resources/episode-2/)
   - [Episode 3: Early Chemical Evolution](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-8-007-cosmic-origin-of-the-chemical-elements-fall-2019/resources/episode-3-early-chemical-evolution/)
   Draw three boxes for an earlier star, surrounding gas and a later star. Add arrows for the movement of material. List a measurement of the later star that could constrain one arrow but leave another uncertain.
3. What evidence could identify an early episode of chemical enrichment?
   - [Episode 3: Early Chemical Evolution](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-8-007-cosmic-origin-of-the-chemical-elements-fall-2019/resources/episode-3-early-chemical-evolution/)
   - [Episode 4: The First Chemical Enrichment Events](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-8-007-cosmic-origin-of-the-chemical-elements-fall-2019/resources/episode-4-the-first-chemical-enrichment-events/)
   Imagine two gas clouds with equal amounts of iron and different ratios of two other elements. Give a mixing history and a production history that might distinguish them. Specify an observation that would help choose.
