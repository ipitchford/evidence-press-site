# Read the record of a river

For geology learners interpreting a sedimentary outcrop. Reconstruct transport, bed shape and preservation before assigning a flow direction.

1. When does flowing water carry sediment in suspension?

   - [Lecture 3: Bed Forms](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-12-003-fluid-motions-sediment-transport-and-current-generated-sedimentary-structures-fall-2025/resources/mitres_12-003f25_lec3_1080p_mp4/)
   Draw a cross-section of a channel and propose two sampling heights. Explain which part of the sediment load each might miss. Decide what else you need to estimate a transport rate.
2. How can layers of rock record the direction of an ancient current?
   - [Lecture 3: Bed Forms](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-12-003-fluid-motions-sediment-transport-and-current-generated-sedimentary-structures-fall-2025/resources/mitres_12-003f25_lec3_1080p_mp4/)
   - [Lecture 4: Sedimentary Structures Produced by Sediment Transport](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-12-003-fluid-motions-sediment-transport-and-current-generated-sedimentary-structures-fall-2025/resources/mitres_12-003f25_lec4_1080p_mp4/)
   Draw three overlapping positions of a migrating dune. Shade what a later dune removes. From the surviving inclined layers, infer one direction and identify one ambiguity.
