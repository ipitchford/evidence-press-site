# Trace light through an instrument

For optics students who can sketch rays. Separate the direction of a ray, its phase and its contribution to an image.

1. Why do thin films show different colours at different angles?
   - [Thin Film Rainbows](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-3-004-visualizing-materials-science-fall-2017/resources/thin-film-rainbows/)
   - [Lecture 2: Reflection and refraction; prisms, waveguides, and dispersion](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-71-optics-spring-2009/resources/lecture-2-reflection-and-refraction-prisms-waveguides-and-dispersion/)
   Sketch the two reflected rays. Predict which features would change if the film thickness varied across the surface, then distinguish that prediction from changing the angle at a single point.
2. Where does a lens form an image?
   - [Lecture 3: Focusing, imaging, and the paraxial approximation](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-71-optics-spring-2009/resources/lecture-3-focusing-imaging-and-the-paraxial-approximation/)
   - [Lecture 4: Sign conventions; thin lenses; real and virtual images](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-71-optics-spring-2009/resources/lecture-4-sign-conventions-thin-lenses-real-and-virtual-images/)
   Draw rays from two object points through a converging lens. Move the object inside the focal length, extend the outgoing rays backwards and record where their extensions meet.
3. How can a ray matrix predict the path through a camera lens?
   - [Lecture 2: Modern optics and lenses; ray-matrix operations; context enhanced imaging - Part 1](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/mas-531-computational-camera-and-photography-fall-2009/resources/mitmas_531f09_lec02_1/)
   - [Lecture 2: Modern optics and lenses; ray-matrix operations; context enhanced imaging - Part 2](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/mas-531-computational-camera-and-photography-fall-2009/resources/mitmas_531f09_lec02_2/)
   Draw a ray through an air gap followed by a thin lens. Reverse the order, keeping the input ray fixed. Explain which intermediate height changes and why the final ray need not coincide.
