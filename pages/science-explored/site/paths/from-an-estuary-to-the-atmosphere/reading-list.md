# From an estuary to the atmosphere

For Earth science students tracing material across system boundaries. Distinguish mixing, gas transfer and the longer cycle.

1. When does mixing fail to explain estuary chemistry?
   - [Session 10: Non-conservative Processes in Estuaries/ Groundwater/Hydrothermal](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/12-742-marine-chemistry-fall-2006/resources/mit12_742f06_ses10_mp4/)
   - [Session 11: Gas Exchange Across the Air-sea Interface](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/12-742-marine-chemistry-fall-2006/resources/mit12_742f06_ses11_mp4/)
   Plot two end members on salinity and concentration axes. Join them, add a sample above the line, and give two explanations for the departure. What third measurement would separate them?
2. What sets the speed of air–sea gas exchange?
   - [Session 11: Gas Exchange Across the Air-sea Interface](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/12-742-marine-chemistry-fall-2006/resources/mit12_742f06_ses11_mp4/)
   - [Session 12: Atmospheric-ocean interaction](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/12-742-marine-chemistry-fall-2006/resources/mit12_742f06_ses12_mp4/)
   Sketch concentration against time for two stirred tanks approaching the same final concentration at different rates. Label a measurement that would distinguish them before either reaches equilibrium.
3. What changes when an energy model moves from the planet to one house?
   - [Session 1-1: A Systems View of the Energy Transition and Role of Methane Emissions](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-env-007-geothermal-energy-networks-transforming-our-thermal-energy-system-january-iap-2025/resources/geothermal-session-1-1_mp4/)
   - [Session 1-2: Carbon Reduction Pathways for Building Stocks](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-env-007-geothermal-energy-networks-transforming-our-thermal-energy-system-january-iap-2025/resources/geothermal-session-1-2_mp4/)
   Draw a global emissions boundary and a building boundary. Place electricity generation, a heat pump and building insulation in each diagram. Identify one reduction that could disappear when the boundary is widened.
