Learning path

Follow energy through a device

For engineering students learning to choose a system boundary. Account for heat, carrier generation and electrical output.

  1. Can two processes transfer different amounts of heat between the same states?

    Draw two paths on a pressure–volume plot between the same endpoints. Compare the areas under the curves. For a closed system with only pressure–volume work, explain what must compensate for their difference.

    Lecture 3: Process Variables and the First Law · Lecture 5: Second Law and Entropy Maximization

  2. How does a solar cell turn light into current?

    Draw an illuminated piece of silicon with no terminals attached. Add a junction and contacts in a second sketch. Mark which arrows describe the creation of carriers and which describe their separation or collection.

    Tutorial: Photoconductivity · Tutorial: Solar Cell Operation

  3. When can an entire circuit be replaced by two components?

    Draw a source feeding a two-resistor divider. Calculate its open-circuit output and its output with a third resistor as a load. Write down the two terminal measurements your replacement must reproduce.

    Lecture 2: Basic Circuit Analysis Method · Lecture 3: Superposition, Thévenin and Norton
