Reading an ocean ridge

For learners meeting plate tectonics for the first time. Use a magnetic profile to make a prediction and design an independent check.

  1. Read the observations

    Sketch the magnetic pattern before proposing a mechanism.

  2. Watch the ridge grow

    Track the position and magnetic direction of one band.

  3. Choose a test

    Explain which independent measurement could discriminate between two explanations.
