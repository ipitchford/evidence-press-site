# From a coordinate choice to a conservation law

For physics students moving from Newtonian mechanics to relativity. Distinguish a choice of origin from a measurable change in momentum.

1. When does a force change angular momentum?
   - [A review of Newton's three laws of motion](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-003sc-engineering-dynamics-fall-2011/resources/a-review-of-newtons-three-laws-of-motion/)
   - [Angular momentum of a particle, torque as the time rate of change of angular momentum, and the appearance of the coriolis force](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-003sc-engineering-dynamics-fall-2011/resources/angular-momentum-of-a-particle-torque-as-the-time-rate-of-change-of-angular-momentum-and-the-appearance-of-the-coriolis-force/)
   Draw three forces of equal magnitude acting on a wheel at different locations and angles. Choose the axle as origin, rank the torques, then change the origin and explain why your calculation changes.
2. Why can two observers disagree about simultaneity?
   - [Lecture 1.4: Space, Time, and Spacetime](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-20-introduction-to-special-relativity-january-iap-2021/resources/lecture-1-2/)
   - [Lecture 3: The Doppler Effect and Special Relativity](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-286-the-early-universe-fall-2013/resources/lecture-3-the-doppler-effect-and-special-relativity/)
   Draw two lightning flashes, the light paths to a midpoint and the path of a moving observer. Label emission, reception and the inferred times. Identify the synchronisation assumption behind the inference.
3. How can a conservation law survive a change of coordinates?
   - [Lecture 2: Introduction to Tensors](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-962-general-relativity-spring-2020/resources/lecture-2-introduction-to-tensors/)
   - [Lecture 4: Volumes and Volume Elements; Conservation Laws](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-962-general-relativity-spring-2020/resources/lecture-4-volumes-and-volume-elements-conservation-laws/)
   Draw a fixed displacement and two rotated coordinate frames. Give two sets of components for the same arrow. Explain why comparing one component across the frames can suggest a spurious change.
