# Read a quantitative paper critically

1. [Lecture 07 : Variables and Types of Data](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/111104120) — List each main variable, how it was obtained and the proposed interpretation.
2. [Lecture 08 : Absolute Frequency, Relative Frequency and Frequency Distribution](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/111104120) — Write the numerator, denominator and missing-data count for one reported proportion, or mark the missing information.
3. [Lecture 18 : Range, Interquartile Range and Quartile Deviation](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/111104120) — Find a spread measure and state whether it describes individual variation or uncertainty in an estimate.
4. [Lecture 25 : Univariate and Bivariate Scatter Plots](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/111104120) — Sketch what the scatterplot would need to reveal, including unusual points or separate groups.
5. [Hypothesis testing of variance and proportions](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/103106120) — Decide whether the reported test concerns a mean, a variance or a proportion; record its assumptions rather than guessing from the p-value.
6. [p value](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/103106120) — Rewrite the p-value sentence without assigning a probability that the hypothesis is true.
7. [Hypothesis testing using confidence interval](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/103106120) — State which effect sizes remain compatible with the reported uncertainty under the chosen method.
8. [Statistic for linear regression](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/103106120) — List the outcome, predictors and one error-model assumption; label a causal claim separately.
9. [Hypothesis testing in linear regression](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/103106120) — Identify whether an interval refers to a fitted mean or a new case, and what evidence of out-of-sample performance is available.
10. [Statistics for Hypothesis Testing - Part 1](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/103106120) — Write a five-sentence appraisal: observation, estimate, uncertainty, assumption and unresolved reporting item. Consult TOP before claiming the result is reproducible.
