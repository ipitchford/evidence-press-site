MIT 22.67J Principles of Plasma Diagnostics, Fall 2023

  1. Lecture 1: Introduction to Principles of Plasma Diagnostics

    Why we need plasma diagnostics, and what sort of plasmas we are interested in.

  2. Lecture 2: Magnetics I

    Basics of magnetic probes and Rogowksi coils.

  3. Lecture 3: Magnetics II

    Advanced magnetic probes, for mode reconstruction and measuring energy confinement time.

  4. Lecture 4: Langmuir Probe

    Basics of Langmuir probes; saturation current, swept probes, double and triple probes.

  5. Lecture 5: Refractive Index Diagnostics I

    Cold plasma refractive index. Refraction and Schlieren imaging.

  6. Lecture 6: Refractive Index Diagnostics II

    Shadowgraphy and deflection potential reconstruction.

  7. Lecture 7: Refractive Index Diagnostics III

    Interferometry, and temporal heterodyning.

  8. Lecture 8: Refractive Index Diagnostics IV

    Spatial heterodyning of interferometry, two-color interferometry.

  9. Lecture 9: Refractive Index Diagnostics V: Abel and Faraday

    Techniques for Abel inverting data, and the cold plasma dispersion in a magnetized plasma.

  10. Lecture 10: Refractive Index Diagnostics VI: Faraday and Reflectometry

    Faraday rotation and reflectometry.

  11. Lecture 11: Radiation

    Radiation transport, radiation from moving charges.

  12. Lecture 12: Electron Cyclotron Emission

    ECE emission and O and X mode accessibility.

  13. Lecture 13: CECE and Bolometry

    Correlation ECE, bremsstrahlung and recombination emission and bolometry.

  14. Lecture 14: Line Radiation

    Atomic models for spectra, different equilibria.

  15. Lecture 15: Equilibria and Imaging

    Modelling spectra X-ray imaging, filters, and detectors.

  16. Lecture 16: Line Broadening

    Natural, Doppler, Stark and Zeeman mechanisms for line broadening.

  17. Lecture 17: Proton Imaging

    Proton imaging and radiography, and the reconstruction of E and B fields from deflections.

  18. Lecture 18: Neutral Particles

    Mechanisms for neutral particle production in the core or actively, and their detection.

  19. Lecture 19: Nuclear Diagnostics

    Guest lecture by Dr. Maria Gatu-Johnson on nuclear diagnostics at OMEGA, NIF, and Z.

  20. Lecture 20: Thomson Scattering: Basics

    Scattering of waves by individual charged particle.

  21. Lecture 21: Thomson Scattering: Non-Collective

    Incoherent scattering by a distribution of charged particles, beginning of the derivation of coherent scattering.

  22. Lecture 22: Thomson Scattering: Collective

    More on coherent scattering.

  23. Lecture 23: Thomson Scattering: Advanced

    Applications of coherent scattering and advanced topics such as electron-ion drift, finite numerical aperture, finite volume, and magnetic fields.
