MIT Chemistry Behind the Magic

  1. Money to Burn

    The old adage takes on new meaning… with the help of a little flash paper and isopropanol. (No money was harmed during the making of this video.) Teaching Notes: Burning a $1.00 Bill (PDF) Chemistry Magician: Dr. Bassam Shakhashiri

  2. Death of a Gummy Bear

    A gummy bear may not be big, but its few grams of sugar contain a lot of energy. Find out how much by combusting one lone gummy in a test tube. Teaching Notes: Toasting a Gummy Candy (PDF) Chemistry Magician: Dr. John Dolhun

  3. Midas' Magic

    Mix these two clear and colorless liquids—that both look just like water—and the result is anything but clear and colorless. Teaching Notes: Midas’ Magic (PDF) Chemistry Magician: Dr. Bassam Shakhashiri

  4. Mirror Mirror

    Mix two mystery ingredients with some table sugar, and the result is… mirror, mirror, fairest of them all. Teaching Notes: Silver Water: Creating a Silver Mirrored Flask (PDF) Chemistry Magician: Dr. John Dolhun

  5. Colorful Indicators

    Colorful indicators tell the story of acids, bases, and the places in between. Teaching Notes: Acidic Properties of CO2 in Aqueous Solutions (PDF) Chemistry Magician: Dr. Bassam Shakhashiri

  6. Anatomy of a Glowstick

    Discover the colorful chemistry that makes a good party glow. Teaching Notes: Chemiluminescence: What’s Inside a Light Stick Anyway? (PDF) Chemistry Magician: Dr. John Dolhun

  7. The Steaming Gun

    In a dark bottle at room temperature, hydrogen peroxide decomposes very, very slowly. But add manganese dioxide, and that same reaction happens thousands of times faster, with very… tall results. Teaching Notes: Chemical Transformation I: Decomposition with H2

  8. Bringin' Home the Bacon

    Dr. John Dolhun is hungry. But the bacon he’s got on the griddle isn’t for him. It’s for Bromine. Teaching Notes: Toxic Compunds from Cooking “Cracking” Bacon Fat (PDF) Chemistry Magician: Dr. John Dolhun

  9. Elements on Fire

    Discover what happens when you burn different elements of the periodic table… Teaching Notes: Simulate the Colors in Fireworks (PDF) Chemistry Magician: Dr. John Dolhun

  10. Let It Snow

    Discover what disposable diapers and fake snow have in common. Teaching Notes: Superabsorbent Polymers (PDF) Chemistry Magician: Dr. John Dolhun

  11. Briggs-Rauscher Reaction

    Mix these three colorless liquids and you get a chemical reaction that can tell time. Teaching Notes: The Splendor of One Chemical Reaction (PDF) Chemistry Magician: Dr. John Dolhun

  12. Let's Drink To That

    Dr. John Dolhun is thirsty. But when he asks his assistant for a drink, he gets more than he bargained for… Teaching Notes: Water to Wine to Milk to Beer (PDF) Chemistry Magician: Dr. John Dolhun

  13. Science is Fun and the Joy of Learning
