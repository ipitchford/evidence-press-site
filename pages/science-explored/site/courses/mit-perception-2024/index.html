  2. 9: Lateral Geniculate Nucleus (LGN) / Primary Visual Cortex (V1)

    Connects retinal and cortical receptive fields with visual population codes. The inspected opening compares centre–surround and orientation-selective responses, then uses the tilt aftereffect to motivate a population-response hypothesis.

  3. 14: Motion Perception (cont'd)

    Examines how local visual-motion measurements can be combined. The inspected opening reviews delayed-input detectors and the aperture problem, then compares component- and pattern-motion responses using plaid stimuli and V1/MT examples.
