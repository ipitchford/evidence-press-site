MIT 2.71 Optics, Spring 2009

  1. Lecture 1: Course organization; introduction to optics

    Topics : Introduction; brief history of optics; absorption, refraction; laws of reflection and refraction. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  2. Lecture 2: Reflection and refraction; prisms, waveguides, and dispersion

    Topics : Laws of reflection and refraction; prisms; dispersion; paraboloidal reflector. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  3. Lecture 3: Focusing, imaging, and the paraxial approximation

    Topics : Perfect focusing; paraboloidal reflector; ellipsoidal refractor; introduction to imaging; perfect on-axis imaging using aspheric lenses; imperfect imaging using spherical surfaces; paraxial approximation; ray transfer matrices. Instructors : George Ba

  4. Lecture 4: Sign conventions; thin lenses; real and virtual images

    Topics : Sign conventions; thin lens; real and virtual images. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  5. Lecture 5: Thick lenses; the composite lens; the eye

    Topics : Imaging at finite distances with thin lenses; thick lenses; the human eye; image formation by a composite lens. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  6. Lecture 6: Terms: apertures, stops, pupils, and windows; single-lens camera

    Topics : Aperture stop; entrance and exit pupils; numerical aperture (NA); field stop; entrance and exit windows; field of view (FoV). Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  7. Lecture 7: Basics of mirrors, magnifiers, and microscopes

    Topics : Ray tracing with mirrors; basic optical systems: single lens magnifier, eyepiece, microscope. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  8. Lecture 8: Telescopes; aberrations: chromatic, spherical, and coma

    Topics : Basic optical systems (cont.): telescope; chromatic aberration; geometrical aberrations: spherical, coma. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  9. Lecture 9: More aberrations; optical design; GRadient INdex (GRIN)

    Topics : Geometrical aberrations (cont.): astigmatism, field curvature, distortion; optical design demo; GRadient INdex (GRIN) optics: quadratic and axial profile; introduction to the Hamiltonian formulation. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  10. Lecture 11: The Hamiltonian formulation; introduction to waves

    Topics : Hamiltonian formulation of ray tracing; analogies between Hamiltonian optics and Hamiltonian mechanics; introduction to waves. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  11. Lecture 12: The wave equation; phasor representation; 3D waves

    Topics : 1D wave equation; complex (phasor) representation; 3D waves: plane, spherical. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  12. Lecture 13: 3D wave phenomena; introduction to electromagnetics

    Topics : 3D waves: plane, spherical; dispersive waves; group velocity; spatial frequencies; introduction to electromagnetics; Maxwell’s equations; derivation of the wave equation for light. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  13. Lecture 14: Maxwell's equations; polarization; Poynting's vector

    Topics : Maxwell’s equations (cont.); polarization justification of the refractive index; electromagnetic energy flux and Poynting’s vector; irradiance (intensity). Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  14. Lecture 15: Huygens principle; interferometers; Fresnel diffraction

    Topics : Interference; Michelson and Mach-Zehnder interferometers; Huygens principle; Young interferometer; Fresnel diffraction. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  15. Lecture 16: Gratings: amplitude and phase, sinusoidal and binary

    Topics : Gratings: amplitude, phase, sinusoidal, binary. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  16. Lecture 17: Fraunhofer diffraction; Fourier transforms and theorems

    Topics : Fraunhofer diffraction; review of Fourier transforms and theorems. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  17. Lecture 18: Spatial filtering; lens transfer functions & transforms

    Topics : Spatial filtering; the transfer function of Fresnel propagation; Fourier transforming properties of lenses. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  18. Lecture 19: The 4F system; binary amplitude & pupil masks

    Topics : 4F system (telescope with finite conjugates) as a cascade of Fourier transforms; binary amplitude and phase pupil masks; Point Spread Function (PSF). Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  19. Lecture 20: Shift invariance; pupil engineering; the Talbot effect

    Topics : Shift invariance; Amplitude Transfer Function (ATF); lateral and angular magnification in the 4F system; relationship between NA, PSF, and ATF; sampling and the Space Bandwidth Product (SBP); advanced spatial filtering: pupil engineering, phase contra

  20. Lecture 22: Coherent and incoherent imaging

    Topics : Temporal and spatial coherence; spatially incoherent imaging; Optical Transfer Function (OTF) and Modulation Transfer Function (MTF); comparison of coherent and incoherent imaging. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  21. Lecture 23: Imaging with a single lens

    Topics : Imaging with a single lens; resolution. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  22. Lecture 25: Resolution; defocused optical systems

    Topics : Resolution (cont.); defocused optical systems. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard

  23. Lecture 26: Depth of focus and field; polarization; wave plates

    Topics : Depth of focus and depth of field; deconvolution and Tikhonov regularization; polarization; wave plates; effects of polarization on high-NA optical systems. Instructors : George Barbastathis, Colin Sheppard
