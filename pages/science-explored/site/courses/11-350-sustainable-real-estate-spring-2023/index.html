MIT 11.350 Sustainable Real Estate, Spring 2023

  1. 11.350 Lecture 2: The Economics of Green Buildings, part 1

    In this video, Prof. Zheng discusses why taking sustainability into consideration in building is a good idea, and how developers can weight the costs and benefits of green building.

  2. 11.350 Lecture 3: The Economics of Green Buildings, Part 2

    In this video, Prof. Zheng discusses the importance of decarbonization in the building sector, what certification programs have been created, and how decarbonization can be achieved.

  3. 11.350 Lecture 5: The Economics of Healthy Buildings

    In this video, Prof. Palacios discusses the difference between green buildings and healthy buildings, and describes various certification schemes for buildings of each type.

  4. 11.350 Lecture 6: 425 Park Ave.

    In this video, Zhengzhen Tan discusses a case study involving the science of healthy buildings and the financial returns of healthy building investment.

  5. 11.350 Lecture 7: From Green Buildings to Green Cities

    In this video, Prof. Zheng discusses how pricing the environmental features of buildings can support decision making.

  6. 11.350 Lecture 8: Climate Risk and Real Estate Markets

    In this video, Prof. Palacios discusses the evidence for risk from different climate-change-related phenomena, and how markets respond to that evidence.

  7. 11.350 Lecture 9: Quantifying Climate Risks’ Impacts on Real Estate Values

    In this video, Profs. Palacios and Zheng discuss how risks associated with climate change have already affected (and will increasingly affect) property values.

  8. 11.350 Lecture 10: Topical Discussions

    In this video, Prof Zheng discusses how climate risks affect real estate investment and commercial and residential property insurance markets.

  9. 11.350 Lecture 11: Policy as a Game Changer for Sustainable Real Estate

    In this video, Prof Zheng discusses ways that national, state, or municipal governments can use “carrot” and “stick” policies to encourage greener buildings.

  10. 11.350 Lecture 13: Financing Sustainable Real Estate at the Asset Level

    In this video, Prof Zheng discusses the rationale for financing green building or retrofitting projects, and how that financing works in practice.

  11. 11.350 Lecture 14: Financing Sustainable Real Estate at the Portfolio Level

    In this video, Prof Zheng discusses real estate investment trusts, green bonds, and ESG benchmarking and disclosure.
