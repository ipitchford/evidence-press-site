MIT 3.054 Cellular Solids: Structure, Properties and Applications, Spring 2015

  1. Lecture 1: Introduction and Overview

    Description: An overview of the course and an introduction to the topic is given in this session. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  2. Lecture 2: Processing of Cellular Solids

    Description: This session covers various ways of processing foams including metal, carbon, ceramics and glass foams, and the structure of cellular materials. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  3. Lecture 3: Structure of Cellular Solids

    Description: The structure of cellular materials, honeycombs and modeling honeycombs are explored in this session. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  4. Lecture 4: Honeycombs: In-plane Behavior

    Description: This session includes a review of honeycombs, and explores the mechanical properties of honeycombs. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  5. Lecture 5: Honeycombs: Out-of-plane Behavior

    Description: Modeling mechanical behavior of honeycombs and out-of-plane properties are discussed. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  6. Lecture 6: Natural Honeycombs: Wood

    Description: This session covers wood structure, micro-structure, stress-strain, honeycomb models, and bending. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  7. Lecture 7: Natural Honeycombs: Cork; Foams: Linear Elasticity

    Description: This session begins with a look at cork as a natural honeycomb structure, and covers properties of foams and some modeling. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  8. Lecture 8: Foams: Non-linear Elasticity

    Description: This session begins with a viewing of video on Hooke’s Micrographia, then covers compressive strength and fracture toughness of foams. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  9. Lecture 9: Foams: Thermal Properties

    Description: Themal properties and behaviors of foams is covered in this session. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  10. Lecture 10: Exam Review

    Description: Professor Gibson takes questions from students in order to review concepts that will be covered on the midterm exam. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  11. Lecture 11: Trabecular Bone and Osteoporosis

    Description: This session covers bone and trabecular bone, and begins discussing osteoporosis. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  12. Lecture 12: Osteoporosis and Evolution

    Description: This session continues on osteoporosis and covers bone structure related to evolution. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  13. Lecture 13: Tissue Engineering Scaffolds: Processing and Properties

    Description: This session covers fabrication, microstructure and mechanical properties of osteochondral scaffold. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  14. Lecture 14: Tissue Engineering: Osteochondral Scaffold; How To Write a Paper

    Description: This session covers cell-scaffold interaction, degradation, cell attachment, morphology, contractility, migration and differentiation. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  15. Lecture 15: Cell-scaffold Interactions; Energy Absorption

    Description: This session covers scaffold interactions, degradation, cell adhesion, wound and scar formation, and energy absorption mechanics. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  16. Lecture 16: Applications: Energy Absorption in Foams

    Description: This session covers more on energy absorption of foams, and continues with modelling, concluding with a discussion of bicycle helmets and woodpeckers. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  17. Lecture 17: Sandwich Panels

    Description: This session introduces sandwich panels, stress, strain, failure modes and optimum design. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  18. Lecture 18: Natural Sandwich Structures; Density Gradients

    Description: Discussion of sandwich panels continues with natural sandwich structures and density gradients. Instructor: Lorna Gibson

  19. Lecture 19: Biomimicking

    Description: Review of materials with biomimicking, and final exam review. Instructor: Lorna Gibson
