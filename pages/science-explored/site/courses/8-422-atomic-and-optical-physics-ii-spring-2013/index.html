MIT 8.422 Atomic and Optical Physics II, Spring 2013

  1. Lecture 1: Introduction to Atomic Physics

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed “What is AMO physics?”, “How has AMO physics developed?”, etc. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  2. Lecture 2: QED Hamiltonian

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed QED Hamiltonian starting from electromagnetism. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  3. Lecture 3: Quantum description of light, Part 1

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed single mode light, thermal states, coherent states, etc. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  4. Lecture 3: Quantum description of light, Part 2

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed fluctuations and noise, and the single photon. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  5. Lecture 4: Non-classical light, squeezing, Part 1

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed classical squeezing, squeezed quantum states, the displacement operator and electric field of squeezed states. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  6. Lecture 4: Non-classical light, squeezing, Part 2

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed Homodyne detection and teleportation of light. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  7. Lecture 5: Single photons, Part 1

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed phase shifters and beam splitters, Mach-Zehnder interferometer, etc. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  8. Lecture 5: Single photons, Part 2

    Description: In this lecture, the professor continued to talk about single photons, Mach-Zehnder interferometer, etc. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  9. Lecture 6: Entangled states

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed entanglement, entangled states, Bell inequality, etc. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  10. Lecture 7: Metrology, shot noise and Heisenberg limit, Part 1

    Description: In this lecture, the professor continued to talk about Bell inequality and discussed quantum metrology. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  11. Lecture 7: Metrology, shot noise and Heisenberg limit, Part 2

    Description: In this lecture, the professor first talked about “Ion trapped in a solid”, then discussed metrology, shot noise and Heisenberg limit. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  12. Lecture 8: g(2) for atoms and light

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed g(2) for atoms and light, classical vs. quantum statistics, etc. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  13. Lecture 9: Diagrams for light-atom interactions

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed Feyman diagrams for light-atom interactions. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  14. Lecture 10: van der Waals and Casimir interactions

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed van der Waals and Casimir interactions. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  15. Lecture 11: Casimir force

    Description: In this video, the professor continued to talk about Casimir interactions and discussed Casimir force. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  16. Lecture 12: Resonant interactions

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed Resonant scattering. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  17. Lecture 13: Derivation of optical Bloch equations

    Description: In this lecture, the professor focused on the derivation of optical Bloch equations. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  18. Lecture 14: Solutions of optical Bloch equations, Part 1

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed spectrum and intensity of emitted light. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  19. Lecture 14: Solutions of optical Bloch equations, Part 2

    Description: In this video, the professor discussed steady state solutions. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  20. Lecture 15: Unraveling Open System Quantum Dynamics

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed motivation for single quantum systems, QMCWF, and models for dephasing. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  21. Lecture 16: Light forces, Part 1

    Description: In this video, the professor discussed light forces, mechanical forces, radiation pressure force, reactive forces. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  22. Lecture 16: Light forces, Part 2

    Description: In this video, the professor discussed applications of the spontaneous force. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  23. Lecture 17: Dressed atom, Part 1

    Description: In this video, the professor discussed combined state of atom and electromagnetic field, dressed basis, laser field, etc. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  24. Lecture 17: Dressed atom, Part 2

    Description: In this video, the professor discussed dipole traps, energy conservation, electric vs. magnetic forces, etc. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  25. Lecture 18: Techniques for ultralow temperatures

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed magnetic trapping and evaporative cooling. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  26. Lecture19: Bose gases

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed ideal Bose gases, weakly interacting homogenous Bose gas, inhomogenous Bose gas, etc. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  27. Lecture 20: Fermi gases, BEC-BCS crossover

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed cold fermions, cooper pairing, experimental realization of the BEC-BCS Crossover, etc. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  28. Lecture 21: Ion trapping and quantum gates

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed ion traps, quantum computation with ions, etc. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle
