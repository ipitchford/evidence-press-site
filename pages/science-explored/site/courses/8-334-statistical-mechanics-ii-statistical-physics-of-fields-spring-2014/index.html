Statistical Mechanics II: Statistical Physics of Fields

MIT OpenCourseWare · 26 catalogue entries

MIT OpenCourseWare · Spring 2014

Original course and syllabus ↗

MIT 8.334 Statistical Mechanics II, Spring 2014

  1. Lecture 1: Collective Behavior, from Particles to Fields Part 1

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar introduces the principles of collective behavior from particles to fields, including Phonons and Elasticity. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  2. Lecture 2: Lec 1 (continued); The Landau-Ginzburg Approach Part 1

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion of the principles of collective behavior from particles to fields, and introduces the Landau-Ginzburg Approach. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  3. Lecture 3: The Landau-Ginzburg Approach Part 2

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion of The Landau-Ginzburg Approach, including Spontaneous Symmetry Breaking and Goldstone Modes. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  4. Lecture 4: The Landau-Ginzburg Approach Part 3

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion of The Landau-Ginzburg Approach, including Scattering and Fluctuations, Correlation Functions and Susceptibilities, Comparison to Experiments. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  5. Lecture 5: The Landau-Ginzburg Approach Part 4

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion of The Landau-Ginzburg Approach, including Gaussian Integrals, Fluctuation Corrections to the Saddle Point, The Ginzburg Criterion. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  6. Lecture 6: The Scaling Hypothesis Part 1

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar introduces the Scaling Hypothesis, including the Homogeneity Assumption, Divergence of the Correlation Length, Critical Correlation Functions and Self-similarity. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  7. Lecture 7: The Scaling Hypothesis Part 2

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion of The Scaling Hypothesis, including the Renormalization Group (Conceptual), and the Renormalization Group (Formal). Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  8. Lecture 8: The Scaling Hypothesis Part 3

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion of The Scaling Hypothesis, including the Gaussian Model (Direct Solution), The Gaussian Model (Renormalization Group). Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  9. Lecture 9: Perturbative Renormalization Group Part 1

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar introduces the Perturbative Renormalization Group, including the Expectation Values in the Gaussian Model, Expectation Values in Perturbation Theory, Diagrammatic Representation of Perturbation Theory, and Susceptibil

  10. Lecture 10: Perturbative Renormalization Group Part 2

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion on the Perturbative Renormalization Group, including Perturbative RG (First Order). Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  11. Lecture 11: Perturbative Renormalization Group Part 3

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion on the Perturbative Renormalization Group, including Perturbative RG (Second Order), and the ε-expansion. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  12. Lecture 12: Perturbative Renormalization Group Part 4

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion on the Perturbative Renormalization Group, including the Irrelevance of Other Interactions and comments on the ε-expansion. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  13. Lecture 13: Position Space Renormalization Group, Part 1

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar introduces the Perturbative Renormalization Group, including Lattice Models, and Exact Treatment in d=1. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  14. Lecture 14: Position Space Renormalization Group, Part 2

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar introduces the Perturbative Renormalization Group, including the Niemeijer-van Leeuwen Cumulant Approximation and the Migdal-Kadanoff Bond Moving Approximation. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  15. Lecture 15: Series Expansions Part 1

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar introduces Series Expansions, including Low-temperature Expansions, High-temperature Expansions, and Exact Solution of the One Dimensional Ising Model. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  16. Lecture 16: Series Expansions Part 2

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion on Series Expansions, including Self-duality in the Two Dimensional Ising Model and Dual of the Three Dimensional Ising Model. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  17. Lecture 17: Series Expansions Part 3

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion on Series Expansions, including Summing over Phantom Loops. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  18. Lecture 18: Series Expansions Part 4

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion on Series Expansions, including Exact Free Energy of the Square Lattice Ising Model. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  19. Lecture 19: Series Expansions Part 5

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion on Series Expansions, including Critical Behavior of the Two Dimensional Ising Model. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  20. Lecture 20: Continuous Spins at Low Temperatures Part 1

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar introduces Continuous Spins at Low Temperatures, including the Non-linear σ-model. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  21. Lecture 21: Continuous Spins at Low Temperatures Part 2

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion on Continuous Spins at Low Temperatures, including Topological Defects in the XY Model. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  22. Lecture 22: Continuous Spins at Low Temperatures Part 3

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion on Continuous Spins at Low Temperatures, including Renormalization Group for the Coulomb Gas. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  23. Lecture 23: Continuous Spins at Low Temperatures Part 4

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion on Continuous Spins at Low Temperatures, including Two Dimensional Solids and Two Dimensional Melting. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  24. Lecture 24: Dissipative Dynamics

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar introduces Dissipative Dynamics, including Brownian Motion of a Particle. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  25. Lecture 25: Continuous Spins at Low Temperatures Part 5

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion on Continuous Spins at Low Temperatures, including Equilibrium Dynamics of a Field and Dynamics of a Conserved Field. Instructor: Prof. Mehran Kardar

  26. Lecture 26: Continuous Spins at Low Temperatures Part 6

    Description: In this lecture, Prof. Kardar continues his discussion on Continuous Spins at Low Temperatures, including Generic Scale Invariance in Equilibrium Systems, Non-equilibrium Dynamics of Open Systems, and Dynamics of a Growing Surface. Instructor: Pro
