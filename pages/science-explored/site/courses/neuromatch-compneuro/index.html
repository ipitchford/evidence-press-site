Neuromatch Academy: Computational Neuroscience

Neuromatch Academy — Computational Neuroscience · 16 catalogue entries

Neuromatch Academy — Computational Neuroscience ·

Original course and syllabus ↗

  1. Intro

    Introduces the biological complexity behind neuron and synapse models, including electrical signalling and the route into simplified computational descriptions.

  2. Intro

    Neuromatch’s Model Types introduction distinguishes descriptive, mechanistic and principle-seeking modelling goals using neuronal inter-spike intervals as a shared example.

  3. Video 1: Dynamic networks

    Introduces rate-based neural networks and an input-to-firing-rate relation for a single population of neurons.

  4. Video 1: Fixed points and their stability

    Extends the two-population model to fixed points, local stability, oscillatory behaviour and inhibition-stabilised regimes.

  5. Video 1: Input & output correlations

    Connects correlated inputs with correlations in neuronal outputs, supported by exercises using pairs of simplified spiking neurons.

  6. Video 1: Linear Regression & Mean Squared Error

    Introduces linear regression and mean squared error within a Neuromatch tutorial that asks learners to calculate error and fit a model parameter.

  7. Video 1: Phase analysis of the Wilson-Cowan E-I model

    Introduces the Wilson–Cowan model of interacting excitatory and inhibitory populations and its two-dimensional phase plane.

  8. Video 1: Reduced Neuron Models

    Introduces the reduction of a biological neuron to a leaky integrate-and-fire model, paired with a written explanation of its assumptions and a coding exercise.

  9. Video 1: STDP

    Introduces a synaptic learning model in which weight changes depend on the relative timing of pre- and postsynaptic spikes.

  10. Video 1: Static and dynamic synapses

    Introduces static and dynamic synapses, with a notebook that contrasts fixed connection strengths with short-term facilitation and depression.

  11. Video 1: “What” Models

    Introduces the descriptive-model section of a Neuromatch tutorial that explores spike recordings, visualisation and inter-spike-interval distributions.

  12. Video 2: Fixed point

    Introduces recurrent networks, fixed points and a one-dimensional phase description of changing activity.

  13. Video 2: Nullclines and Vector Fields

    Uses nullclines and vector fields to describe the directions in which excitatory and inhibitory population activity changes.

  14. Video 2: Response of ensemble of neurons to time-varying input

    A bonus lecture on how an ensemble of neurons responds to input that varies over time, following a tutorial about shared inputs and correlations.

  15. Video 2: Response of the LIF neuron to different inputs

    Introduces responses of a leaky integrate-and-fire neuron to different inputs, alongside demonstrations using constant and noisy currents.

  16. Video 3: Stability of fixed points

    Explains local stability by considering whether a small perturbation around a fixed point grows or decays.
