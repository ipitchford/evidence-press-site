Seafloor Spreading and Marine Magnetic Anomalies

University of California, Santa Barbara · 3 catalogue entries

University of California, Santa Barbara ·

Original course and syllabus ↗

  1. Magnetic polarity reversal explanation

    Read the reversal timescale from youngest to oldest before applying it to the crust.

  2. Seafloor spreading with magnetic stripes and reversal scale

    Track one strip of crust against the polarity timescale. The model adds crust at three ridge segments linked by transform faults.

  3. Seafloor spreading with magnetic stripes

    Compare the stripe sequence across the ridge. Then follow one stripe towards an offset in the ridge axis.
