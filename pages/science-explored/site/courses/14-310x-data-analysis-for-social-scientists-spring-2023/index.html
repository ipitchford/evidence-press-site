Original course and syllabus ↗

MIT 14.310x Data Analysis for Social Scientists, Spring 2023

  1. Lecture 01: Introduction to 14.310x Data Analysis for Social Scientists

    In this first lecture, Esther Duflo introduces the course and talks about how data can be powerful, beautiful, and also deceitful.

  2. Lecture 02: Fundamentals of Probability

    In this lecture, Sara Ellison begins the discussion of probability, discussing the fundamentals, conditional probability, and independence.

  3. Lecture 03: Random Variables, Distributions, and Joint Distributions

    In this lecture, Sara Ellison begins by talking about random variables and continues with the concept of distributions and joint distributions.

  4. Lecture 04: Gathering and Collecting Data

    In this video, Esther Duflo covers where to find data, visualizing distributions, and using the distribution function to extract the knowledge we want.

  5. Lecture 05: Summarizing and Describing Data

    In this video, Esther Duflo discusses kernel density estimation, cumulative distribution functions, and histograms.

  6. Lecture 06: Joint, Marginal, and Conditional Distributions

    In this video, Sara Ellison continues the discussion of joint distributions and then covers independence of random variables and conditional distribution.

  7. Lecture 07: Functions of Random Variables

    In this video, Sara Ellison discusses probability density function (PDF) and cumulative distribution function (CDF) of random variables, including examples.

  8. Lecture 08: Moments of Distribution

    In this video, Sara Ellison continues the discussion of functions, then moves onto moments of distribution, and concludes with an extended example about auction theory.

  9. Lecture 09: Expectation, Variance, and Introduction to Regression

    In this lecture, Sara Ellison talks about the properties of moments of a distribution and conditional moments.

  10. Lecture 10: Special Distributions

    Esther Duflo begins the lecture by talking about the use of human subjects for research. She then continues the discussion on special distributions.

  11. Lecture 11: Special Distributions, continued. The Sample Mean, Central Limit Theorem, and Estimation

    In this video, Esther Duflo continues the discussion on special distributions from lecture 10. Then Sara Ellison explains the sample mean, Central Limit Theorem, and estimation.

  12. Lecture 12: Assessing and Deriving Estimators

    In this video, Sara Ellison continues to talk about estimation. She also covers criteria for assessing estimators and frameworks for deriving estimators.

  13. Lecture 13. Confidence Intervals, Hypothesis Testing, and Power Calculations

    In this video, Sara Ellison discusses inference, including the topics of confidence intervals and hypothesis testing.

  14. Lecture 14: Causality

    In this video, Esther Duflo explains causality, including different ways to model causality. She then moves on to discuss causality and randomized control trials.

  15. Lecture 15: Analyzing Randomized Experiments

    In this video, Esther Duflo discusses analyzing randomized experiments, as well as, the importance of sample size when designing an experiment.

  16. Lecture 16: (More) Explanatory Data Analysis: Nonparametric Comparisons and Regressions

    In this video, Esther Duflo talks about the uses of randomization in real-life examples and then segues into discussing nonparametric comparisons and regressions.

  17. Lecture 17: The Linear Model

    In this video, Sara Ellison discusses the linear model and estimating it using linear regression.

  18. Lecture 18: The Multivariate Model

    In this video, Sara Ellison begins by giving general pointers on writing an empirical social science paper and gives more details about the students’ projects. She continues by talking about multivariate linear models and estimations.

  19. Lecture 19: Practical Issues in Running Regressions

    In this video, Esther Duflo continues the discussion about testing in the linear model from the last lecture. Next, she talks about practical considerations to consider when running regressions and the regression discontinuity design.

  20. Lecture 20: Omitted Variable Bias

    In this video, Esther Duflo continues the discussion of functional form, regression discontinuity design, and omitted variable bias.

  21. Lecture 21: Endogeneity and Instrument Variables

    In this video, Esther Duflo discusses endogeneity, instrumental variables, and two-stage least squares.

  22. Lecture 22: Experimental Design

    In this video, Esther Duflo continues the discussion of instrumental variables from Lecture 21. She then moves on to discuss experimental design.
