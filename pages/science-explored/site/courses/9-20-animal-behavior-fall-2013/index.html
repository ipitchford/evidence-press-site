MIT 9.20 Animal Behavior, Fall 2013

  1. Lec1: Introduction to animal behavior and how it is studied

    Description: This lecture covers the layout and organization of the course as well as introducing approaches to studying animal behavior. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  2. Lec2: Introduction to ethology

    Description: This lecture provides an introduction to ethology as well as discussing Niko Tinbergen’s four questions and three-spined stickleback fish. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  3. Lec4: Ethology and Konrad Lorenz' Jackdaws

    Description: This lecture discusses ethology and specifically uses Konrad Lorenz’ Jackdaws as an example. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  4. Lec6: Fixed action patterns and central nervous system

    Description: This lecture covers fixed action patterns as well as how these innate behaviors are organized in the central nervous system (CNS). Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  5. Lec8: Innate behaviors, motivation and fundamentals of ethology

    Description: This lecture covers innate behaviors and motivation along with the approach of Konrad Lorenz to studying animal behavior and the fundamentals of ethology. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  6. Lec9: Lorenz on fundamental ethology, part 1

    Description: This lecture introduces Konrad Lorenz’ fundamentals of ethology. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  7. Lec10: Lorenz on fundamental ethology, part 2

    Description: This lecture discusses Lorenz’ approaches to ethology. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  8. Lec11: Lorenz on fundamentals of ethology, part 3

    Description: This lecture finishes the discussion of Lorenz’ fundamentals of ethology. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  9. Lec12: Evolution and the development and plasticity of behavior, part 1

    Description: This lecture discusses evolution as well as the development and plasticity of behavior. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  10. Lec13: Evolution and the development and plasticity of behavior, part 2

    Description: This lecture continues with the discussion of evolution as well as the development and plasticity of behavior. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  11. Lec14: Communication and Meerkats of the Kalahari Desert, part 1

    Description: This lecture introduces communication and uses the Meerkats of the Kalahari Desert as an example. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  12. Lec15: Communication and Meerkats of the Kalahari Desert, part 2

    Description: This lecture continues the discussion of communication behaviors and Meerkats. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  13. Lec16: Foraging behaviors

    Description: This lecture discusses foraging behaviors. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  14. Lec17: Anti-predator behavior

    Description: This lecture describes anti-predator behaviors. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  15. Lec18: Mating and reproduction

    Description: This lecture discusses mating behaviors and their effects on reproduction. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  16. Lec20: Sociobiology

    Description: This lecture continues the discussion of sociobiology subject matter, including path analysis. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  17. Lec21: Genetic influences on social behavior

    Description: This lecture discusses the influence of genes/genetics on social behavior, including continuing the discussion of path analyses. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  18. Lec23: Sociobiology, mating behaviors and scientific method

    Description: This lecture continues on the topic of sociobiology with discussions of the domestic cat videos viewed during the previous class, mating behaviors and inherent biases of men and women. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  19. Lec24: Discoveries of sociobiology

    Description: This lecture important discoveries of sociobiology research, including group selection and cryptic female choice behaviors. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  20. Lec25: Cultural determinism and sociobiology

    Description: This lecture continues the discussion of the discoveries of sociobiology. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  21. Lec26: Critique of cultural determinism

    Description: This lecture covers a critique of cultural determinism. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  22. Lec27: Sociobiology and culture

    Description: This lecture discusses the relationships between sociobiology and culture as well as practical issues in sociobiology. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  23. Lec28: Triumph of sociobiology and learning

    Description: This lecture discusses discusses Konrad Lorenz, the Triumph of Sociobiology as well as learning. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  24. Lec29: Konrad Lorenz on learning, part 2

    Description: This lecture continues the discussion of learning as described by Konrad Lorenz, including imprinting and curiosity. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider

  25. Lec30: Konrad Lorenz on learning, part 3

    Description: This lecture concludes the discussion of learning as described by Konrad Lorenz with a focus on motor learning and neuroanatomy. Instructor: Gerald E. Schneider
