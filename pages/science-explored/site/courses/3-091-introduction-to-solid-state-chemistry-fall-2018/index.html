MIT 3.091 Introduction to Solid-State Chemistry, Fall 2018

  1. Lecture 1: Introduction

    Description: This lecture covers which elements comprise specific materials, how these elements interact with one another, how they are structured, and how the material was processed to achieve this structure. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  2. Goodie Bag 1: Atoms and Reactions

  3. Lecture 2: The Periodic Table

    Description: This lecture covers concept of the mole and organizing the periodic table. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  4. Lecture 3: Atomic Models

    Description: This lecture discusses the ground-breaking experiments that brought the scientific community closer to understanding the structure of the atom. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  5. Lecture 4: Atomic Spectra

    Description: This lecture covers the Bohr model and electronic transitions. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  6. Lecture 5: Shell Models and Quantum Numbers

    Description: This lecture continues the discussion of ionization. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  7. Goodie Bag 2: Electronic Transitions

  8. Lecture 6: Electron Shell Model, Quantum Numbers, and PES

    Description: This lecture explores how to find the properties of multiple electrons in an atom using the Schrodinger equation. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  9. Lecture 7: Aufbau Principle and Atomic Orbitals

    Description: This lecture explores the Aufbau principle to remember the order in which subshells are filled in a multielectron atom. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  10. Goodie Bag 3: Ionic Solids

  11. Lecture 8: Ionization Energy and Potential Energy Surface (PES)

    Description: This lecture continues the discussion of ionization energy. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  12. Lecture 9: Lewis Structures I

    Description: This lecture covers Lewis structures, covalent bonds, and resonance. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  13. Lecture 10: Lewis Structures II

    Description: Continuation of Lewis structures, covalent bonds, and resonance. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  14. Lecture 11: Shapes of Molecules and VSEPR

    Description: The VSEPR model allows us to predict the shapes of molecules to more fully understand their properties. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  15. Goodie Bag 4: VSEPR

  16. Lecture 12: Molecular Orbitals

    Description: This lecture describes molecular orbital theory which is used to predict the shape and behavior of electrons shared between atoms. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  17. Lecture 13: Hybridization

    Description: This lecture discusses how multiple atomic orbitals with similar energy levels can combine to form equal orbitals that have a lower average energy. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  18. Lecture 14: Intermolecular Forces

    Description: This session explores how interactions between molecules weaker than ionic or covalent bonds give materials their properties. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  19. Bonus Lecture 1: Phases

    Description: This lecture covers phases, latent heat, and phase diagrams. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  20. Lecture 15: Semiconductors

    Description: This lecture covers conductivity of electrons in semiconductors. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  21. Goodie Bag 5: Electronic Materials

  22. Lecture 16: Doping

    Description: This lecture covers the introduction of impurities to increase the density of charge carriers. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  23. Lecture 17: Metals

    Description: This lecture covers metals which are formed from atoms that have partially-filled electronic bands. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  24. Lecture 18: Introduction to Crystallography

    Description: The arrangement of bonds plays an important role in determining the properties of crystals. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  25. Goodie Bag 6: Crystallography

  26. Lecture 19: Crystallographic Notation

    Description: This lecture covers how identical points are arranged in space in crystalline solids and notation. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  27. Lecture 20: X-ray Emission and Absorption

    Description: This lecture covers x-rays which have the right range of wavelengths to image at the atomic scale. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  28. Lecture 21: X-ray Diffraction Techniques I

    Description: This lecture continues the discussion of x-rays and x-ray diffraction techniques. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  29. Lecture 22: X-ray Diffraction Techniques II

    Description: This lecture continues the discussion of x-ray diffraction techniques. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  30. Lecture 23: Point and Line Defects I

    Description: This lecture covers localized disruptions in the regularity of crystal lattices. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  31. Goodie Bag 7: Defects

  32. Lecture 24: Point and Line Defects II

    Description: This lecture covers line defects, which are one-dimensional defects in a crystal that affect macroscopic materials properties such as deformation. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  33. Lecture 25: Introduction to Glassy Solids

    Description: This lecture describes how atoms of glasses or “amorphous materials” are randomly arranged in a non-repeating structure. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  34. Lecture 26: Engineering Glass Properties

    Description: This lecture explores what it means to engineer glass. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman Guest Speaker: Peter B. Houk

  35. Lecture 27: Reaction Rates

    Description: This lecture explores chemical kinetics. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  36. Goodie Bag 8: Reactions

  37. Lecture 28: Introduction to Aqueous Solutions

    Description: This lecture explores how similar bonds dissolve similar bonds. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  38. Lecture 29: Acids and Bases I

    Description: This lecture explores the common ion effect. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  39. Lecture 30: Acids and Bases II

    Description: This lecture explores the Bronsted-Lowry acids, bases and neutralization. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  40. Lecture 31: Exam Review

    Description: This lecture reviews concepts covered in the exam. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  41. Lecture 32: Polymers I

    Description: Discussion of polymers, radical polymerization, and condensation polymerization. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  42. Bonus Lecture 2: The Chemistry of Batteries

    Description: Discussion of energy storage, electrical storage, and the chemistry of batteries. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  43. Lecture 33: Polymers II

    Description: Discussion of polymer properties and cross linking. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  44. Lecture 34: Introduction to Organic Chemistry

    Description: This lecture covers polymer properties, co-polymers, and nature’s polymers. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  45. Goodie Bag 9: Polymers

  46. Lecture 35: Diffusion I

    Description: This lecture covers steady state and non steady state diffusion. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman

  47. Lecture 36: Diffusion II

    Description: This lecture continues the discussion of steady state and non steady state diffusion. Instructor: Jeffrey C. Grossman
