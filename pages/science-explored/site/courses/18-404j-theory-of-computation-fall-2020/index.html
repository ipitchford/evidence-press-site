  3. Lecture 3: Regular Pumping Lemma, Finite Automata → Regular Expressions, CFGs

  4. Lecture 4: Pushdown Automata, CFG ↔ PDA

    Description: Quickly reviewed last lecture. Defined context free grammars (CFGs) and context free languages (CFLs). Defined pushdown automata (PDA). Gave conversion of CFGs to PDAs. Stated the reverse conversion without proof. Instructor: Prof. Michael Sipser
