MIT 8.421 Atomic and Optical Physics I, Spring 2014

  1. Lecture 1: Resonance I

    Description: This lecture started with the introduction of the classical resonance and provided a general overview of resonance. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  2. Lecture 2: Resonance II

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed harmonic oscillator and precision frequency measurement. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  3. Lecture 3: Resonance III

    Description: In this lecture, the professor reviewed harmonic oscillator, gave two examples about harmonic oscillator and discussed about motion in a rotating magnetic field. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  4. Lecture 4: Resonance IV

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed about quantized spin in a magnetic field and Landau-Zener problem. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  5. Lecture 5: Resonance V and Atoms I

    Description: In this lecture, the professor reviewed Landau-Zener problem; discussed density matrix formalism for arbitrary two-level systems; and started the new chapter “Atoms”. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  6. Lecture 6: Atoms II

    Description: In this lecture, the professor talked about the atomic units, atomic structure, helium atom and energy levels of helium. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  7. Lecture 7: Atoms III

    Description: In this lecture, the professor continued to talk about helium and discussed about fine structure of atoms and lamb shift. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  8. Lecture 8: Atoms IV

    Description: In this lecture, the professor continued to talk about lamb shift and discussed effects of the nucleus on atomic structure. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  9. Lecture 9: Atoms V and Atoms in External Fields I

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed atoms without external fields and atoms in external magnetic fields. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  10. Lecture 10: Atoms in External Fields II

    Description: In this lecture, the professor summarized atoms in external magnetic fields and discussed atoms in external electric fields. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  11. Lecture 11: Atoms in External Fields III

    Description: In this video, the professor reviewed second order perturbation theory and discussed beyond the quadratic Stark effect, field ionization, and atoms in oscillating electric fields. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  12. Lecture 12: Atoms in External Fields IV and Atom-light Interactions I

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed index of refraction and started to talk about atom-light Interactions. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  13. Lecture 13: Atom-light Interactions II

    Description: In this lecture, the professor continued to talk about matrix elements and atom-light Interactions. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  14. Lecture 14: Atom-light Interactions III

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed Einstein’s A and B coefficients and spontaneous emission. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  15. Lecture 15: Atom-light Interactions IV

    Description: In this lecture, the professor first reviewed Einstein’s A and B coefficients and spontaneous emission, then discussed degeneracy factors, fully quantized Hamiltonian, and vacuum Rabi oscillation. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  16. Lecture 16: Atom-light Interactions V

    Description: In this video, the professor reviewed vacuum Rabi oscillation and discussed two-level Hamiltonian. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  17. Lecture 17: Atom-light Interactions VI and Line Broadening I

    Description: In this video, the professor continued to talk about atom-light Interactions and started to discuss line shifts and broadening. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  18. Lecture 18: Line Broadening II

    Description: In this lecture, the professor used simple cases to explain line shifts and broadening. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  19. Lecture19: Line Broadening III

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed doppler broadening, line shape of confined particles, and Dicke narrowing. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  20. Lecture 20: Line Broadening IV and Two-photon Excitation I

    Description: In this lecture, the professor discussed Dicke narrowing, spectrum of emitted light, collisional broadening and two-photon transitions. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  21. Lecture 21: Two-photon Excitation II and Coherence I

    Description: In this lecture, the professor explained some cases of two-photon processes and started to discuss about coherence. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  22. Lecture 22: Coherence II

    Description: In this video, the professor reviewed spontaneous emission and discussed coherence in two-level systems. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  23. Lecture 23: Coherence III

    Description: In this video, the professor discussed coherence in three-level systems. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle

  24. Lecture 24: Coherence IV

    Description: In this video, the professor reviewed dark state transfer and discussed about using AC electric fields to mix S and P states, electromagnetic induced transparency (EIT), Fano profile, two-photon absorption, and slow light. Instructor: Wolfgang Ket

  25. Lecture 25: Coherence V

    Description: In this video, the professor discussed about superradiance. Instructor: Wolfgang Ketterle
