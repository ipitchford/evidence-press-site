Topic guide

How does a solar cell turn light into current?

Start with Tutorial: Photoconductivity

In the photoconductivity tutorial, separate the creation of mobile charge carriers from their movement. An increase in conductivity does not by itself specify which terminal will collect the electrons.

Try it

Draw an illuminated piece of silicon with no terminals attached. Add a junction and contacts in a second sketch. Mark which arrows describe the creation of carriers and which describe their separation or collection.

Check your reasoning

Illumination can create carriers even in material with no preferred collection direction. A working cell must also separate and collect those carriers before they recombine. Conductivity under illumination and electrical power delivered to a load are different measurements.

Continue with Tutorial: Solar Cell Operation

The solar-cell tutorial supplies the missing device structure. Follow the contributions of diffusion and electric fields through it.
