Topic guide

Can two processes transfer different amounts of heat between the same states?

Start with Lecture 3: Process Variables and the First Law

Listen for how a boundary lets energy cross as heat or work. A state measurement can constrain their combined effect while leaving the history of the process open.

Try it

Draw two paths on a pressure–volume plot between the same endpoints. Compare the areas under the curves. For a closed system with only pressure–volume work, explain what must compensate for their difference.

Check your reasoning

For a closed system returning to the same endpoints, the internal-energy change is fixed. With work counted positive when done by the system, the path that does more work must also take in more net heat.

Continue with Lecture 5: Second Law and Entropy Maximization

The second-law lecture adds a question that an energy budget leaves open. Which direction of change is possible?
