Topic guide

Why do thin films show different colours at different angles?

Start with Thin Film Rainbows

Watch for colour changes as viewing geometry changes. Light reflected at the two faces of a film travels along different optical paths. The relative phase depends on the paths and the wavelength.

Try it

Sketch the two reflected rays. Predict which features would change if the film thickness varied across the surface, then distinguish that prediction from changing the angle at a single point.

Check your reasoning

The two reflected waves can reinforce some wavelengths and cancel others. The film thickness, angles within the film and phase changes on reflection affect the result. Geometrical ray directions alone do not determine the observed colour.

Continue with Lecture 2: Reflection and refraction; prisms, waveguides, and dispersion

The optics lecture on reflection and refraction gives the ray directions at each interface. Use those directions to refine the path sketch.
