Topic guide

What makes a feedback signal decay or grow?

Start with Lecture 2: Discrete-Time (DT) Systems

Follow a single value around a delay loop. The delay determines when it returns and the gain determines how much comes back. A block diagram can make both easier to trace than an expanded sequence.

Try it

Start a recurrence at one and multiply its previous value by one half at every step. Repeat with a negative half and with two. Sketch eight samples from each and distinguish decay from oscillation.

Check your reasoning

The negative half alternates sign while its magnitude shrinks at precisely the rate of the positive half. Multiplication by two gives growth. A sign reversal on its own is therefore poor evidence of instability.

Continue with Lecture 3: Feedback, Poles, and Fundamental Modes

The poles lecture shows how several such modes combine. Ask whether one growing mode can dominate several decaying ones.
