Topic guide

When can an entire circuit be replaced by two components?

Start with Lecture 2: Basic Circuit Analysis Method

Begin with the junction and loop equations. They let you predict what an external load will measure without observing every internal current.

Try it

Draw a source feeding a two-resistor divider. Calculate its open-circuit output and its output with a third resistor as a load. Write down the two terminal measurements your replacement must reproduce.

Check your reasoning

For a linear resistive network, the open-circuit voltage fixes one part of the equivalent. The voltage drop under a known load gives the effective series resistance. An internal branch current can differ even when every terminal measurement agrees.

Continue with Lecture 3: Superposition, Thévenin and Norton

Compare your circuit with the Thévenin and Norton forms. Test the replacement on a third load that you did not use to choose its parameters.
