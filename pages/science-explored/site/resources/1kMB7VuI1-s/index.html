Christof Koch -- The Puzzle of Consciousness

Christof Koch introduces the problem of relating subjective experience to descriptions of brain activity.

World Science U

Start a definitions-first consciousness route and distinguish the contents of an experience from a person’s overall state.

Paired summary inspected: first-person/third-person descriptions, the hard problem, conscious content versus state and philosophical framing. The 2015 summary includes categorical clinical claims about unresponsive patients and broad numerical simplifications; these are not adopted as current clinical knowledge.
