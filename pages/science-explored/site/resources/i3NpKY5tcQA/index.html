Functional Connectivity Analysis Methods and Their Interpretational Pitfalls

André Bastos introduces electrophysiological connectivity measures and demonstrates interpretational problems involving common signals, noise, field spread and sample size.

MIT OpenCourseWare

An advanced methods complement to introductory brain imaging: the official tutorial links a review and simulations intended to reveal misleading connectivity results.
