Delayed neuronal maturation in humans

Carol Marchetto examines hypotheses about prolonged neuronal maturation in humans compared with other primates.

CARTA

Connect comparative anatomy with developmental timing while keeping proposed regulatory mechanisms distinct from established observations.

Full session abstract inspected: neoteny, transcriptional regulation, conserved coding sequences and explicitly unresolved mechanisms. The 2023 abstract says key regulatory mechanisms remain unknown; prolonged maturation must not be treated as a complete explanation of human cognitive differences.

Speaker · Carol Marchetto
