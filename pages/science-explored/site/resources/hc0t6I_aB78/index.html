Neural Underpinnings of Individual Recognition in the Brain

Isabella Catalano studies how zebra finches recognise individuals using auditory and visual information.

iBiology

Use a concrete non-human research example to distinguish the behaviour being explained from neural responses measured during a task.

Talk overview inspected: mate calls, learning/memory and reward regions, responses relative to silence and further work still in progress. A 2022 early-career research presentation; implications for human disorders are possibilities, not established translational results.
