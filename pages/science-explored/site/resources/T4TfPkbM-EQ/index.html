CARTA: Love, Loss, and Luminance

Karen Bales compares pair bonds and responses to loss across several species.

CARTA

Move from a proposed biological mechanism to comparative questions about what the relationship does in different animals.

Session abstract inspected: pair bonds, loss, cross-species comparisons and underlying neurobiology. Similarity in a relationship label is not evidence of identical subjective experience or neural mechanisms.
