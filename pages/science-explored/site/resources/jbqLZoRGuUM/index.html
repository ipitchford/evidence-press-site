BI 249 Michael Cole: Brain Flows are the Units of Cognition

Michael Cole discusses a view of cognition centred on flows of activity between brain regions and their network organisation.

Brain Inspired

Compare a network-level proposal with more local or modular explanations and identify the explanatory unit each assumes.

Episode narrative and chapter headings inspected: inter-area flows, compositional hierarchies, cognitive control and proposed links to higher cognition. An interview presenting Cole’s framework, not an independent validation of the book or a complete account of brain function.

Speaker · Michael Cole; Paul Middlebrooks
