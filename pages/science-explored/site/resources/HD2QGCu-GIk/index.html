Beth Stevens (Boston Children’s) 1: Microglia States in Health and Disease

Beth Stevens introduces microglial states and their roles in brain development, health and disease.

iBiology

Establish the cell type and its context-dependent functions before moving to a specific account of synaptic pruning.

The first-talk overview inspected: protective and harmful roles, imaging advances and dynamic states at different developmental stages. An introductory research lecture from 2020; describing a cell’s role does not establish a clinical treatment.
